Description: 

The purpose of this Request for Pre-Qualification is to invite interested parties to submit responses indicating their interests in, and qualifications for, becoming pre-qualified to participate in future procurement opportunities for Architectural services for capital construction projects at Island Health.  This opportunity is open to both new firms as well as firms that have experience with Island Health projects. 
Bid Document Access:  Bid Opportunity notices and awards and a free preview of the bid documents is available on this site free of charge without registration. Please note, some documents may be secured and you will be required to register for the bid to download and view the documents. There is no cost to obtain an unsecured version of the document and /or to participate in this solicitation.
