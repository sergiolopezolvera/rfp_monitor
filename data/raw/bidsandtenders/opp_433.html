The District invites detailed Proposals from construction firms (“Proponents”) with expertise in the design, construction and permitting process for park washrooms utilizing a CCDC14 (2013) design-build stipulated price contract for the provision of a park washroom facility at Handsworth Secondary School Artificial Turf Field (ATF), in North Vancouver, BC.
