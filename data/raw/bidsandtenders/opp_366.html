The Upper Canada District School Board invites tenders, from pre-qualified eligible bidders, for ROOFING & CEILING TILES including

  • Replace roof section B with 2 ply mod bit roofing. 
  • Replace aged roof mounted exhaust units. 
  • Replace existing ceiling tiles with new 2’x4’ suspended ceiling system. 
  • Replace existing corridor lighting. 
  • Provide pad and all services for new portable classroom, 

as further described herein at:

AVONMORE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

 16279 Fairview Drive, Avonmore, ON, K0C 1C0
