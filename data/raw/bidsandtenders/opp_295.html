Notice of Planned Procurements - 2026 (as of February 4, 2026)

For: City of Richmond Hill

This notification is provided to inform suppliers of upcoming planned procurements for the 2026 fiscal year at the City of Richmond Hill.
 
This list is intended to provide vendors with early visibility into the City’s expected contracting opportunities for the upcoming year. The list of planned procurements is subject to change, and the City reserves the right to modify, postpone, or cancel any planned procurement at any time. Project timelines and requirements may also shift based on budget approval, design progress, funding availability, property considerations, or other operational needs.

All formal bid documents for individual procurements will be posted on the City of Richmond Hill’s Bids & Tenders portal, where suppliers will be able to access full details and submit responses.

Important Note:
This notice is for information purposes only. Suppliers are encouraged to create a free Bids & Tenders account to preview upcoming planned procurements and receive notifications once opportunities are officially posted.
Bid Document Access:  Bid document preview, bid opportunity, and award notices are available on the site free of charge. Suppliers are not required to register for a bid opportunity prior to previewing unsecured bid documents. Please note, some documents may be secured and you will be required to register for the bid to download and view the documents. To download secured documents and/or to participate in this opportunity, you will need to have a subscription plan or buy Pay-Per-Bid access for this opportunity (available if you don't have an active subscription).
