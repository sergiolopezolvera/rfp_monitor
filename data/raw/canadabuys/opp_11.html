    Description:
    This requirement is for: House of Commons (HoC)

    This Request for Proposal (RFP) is an invitation by the House of Commons (HoC) to prospective Bidders to submit proposals for the provision, implementation, and support of a comprehensive legal software solution to enhance the management of its legislative, legal, and compliance activities.

    This HoC tender notice and its solicitation documents are available on MERX at the following link: https://www.merx.com/solicitations/open-bids/Legal-Solution-Software/00…

    There is no charge to preview the HoC solicitation documents, noting that in order to do so, you have to at a minimum sign up with MERX selecting the “Free” option.

    You may have received this tender notice through a third-party distributor. The HoC is not responsible for any tender notices and/or related documents and attachments not accessed directly through MERX.
