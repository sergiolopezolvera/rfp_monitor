Invest Alberta Corporation - Negotiated Request for Proposal - Video Production Services

Solicitation number AB-2026-03683

Publication date 2026/05/11

Closing date and time 2026/06/05 10:00 EDT

Create a notification to follow this noticeFollow this notice

    Description
    Invest Alberta Corporation's Marketing and Communications team are seeking proposals from Vendors that are able to provide video production services of various sizes and scales on an as-and-when needed basis with IAC. The successful Proponents will become “preferred vendors” and will be issued a five (5) year Master Services Agreement that IAC may commission in the future. Due to the “as and when needed” nature of this initiative, IAC cannot guarantee any minimum level of expenditure commitment to preferred vendors.

    Contract duration

    The estimated contract period will be 60 month(s), with a proposed start date of 2026/08/01.
