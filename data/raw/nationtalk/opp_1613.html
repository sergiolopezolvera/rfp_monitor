RFP – Curriculum Developer: Indigenous Health Navigator Certification

Tender Information
Author pmnationtalk
Deadline May 18, 2026
Name First Nations Health Managers Association
Type RFP
Region Akwesasne, ON
Email andrea.aiabens@fnhma.ca
Sector Public Sector
Category health
Client First Nations Health Managers Association

REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Curriculum Developer: Indigenous Health Navigator Certification

1. INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND

The First Nations Health Managers Association (FNHMA) provides leadership in First Nations health management activities by developing and promoting quality standards, practices, research, certification, networking, and professional development to expand capacity for our members and First Nations communities.

The Indigenous health system is complex and multi -jurisdictional and requires not just effective health care but also culturally safe health care. The FNHMA has become recognized for its success in supporting community-based health managers who play a key role in strengthening health care for First Nations communities. In addition to health managers, there are other roles that are important in building a more streamlined and culturally safe continuum of care for Indigenous patients and their families, both in community and in urban centers.

Indigenous Health Navigators (IHN) help patients and their families navigate the healthcare system, beyond available community health services. As peer supports, IHN guide patients and families through diagnosis, treatment, and are valuable in helping to access available resources. IHN understand the complex jurisdictional health landscape, the current and historical realities of patients and families, and provide support using a culturally safe and trauma-informed approach. IHN are a bridge between community health services and the mainstream health services that are accessed in urban centres. They are an ongoing resource for patients and families. IHNs have been traditionally based in urban health clinics and hospitals, with opportunities to expand their patient access points to Friendship Centres and other indigenous led organizations.

2. OBJECTIVE

Building on the flagship certification program for First Nations Health Managers, the FNHMA is seeking a Curriculum Developer to develop certified curriculum and training for Indigenous Health Navigators (IHN). Engagement with IHNs, those who manage IHNs, those who work closely or worked in the role previous was completed in March and will be used to help develop the competencies and curriculum moving forward.

This program will reflect the needs of the current Indigenous health work force shown in the engagement report (to be provided). The intent of the work is to support the development of national standard for Indigenous health workers through the development of standardized national competencies that encompass and reflect the range of skills exhibited by this diverse and skilled workforce.

A Prior Learning Assessment and Recognition (PLAR) process will also be developed along side the IHN curriculum development.

3. TIMEFRAME

The project will begin as soon as possible following the selection of the contractor and be completed by March 2027.

4. CONTRACTOR METHODOLOGY

Curriculum Development

Key activities:

  • Draft Core Competencies
  • Validation of Competencies (Calgary, September 22-23, 2026)
  • Draft Curriculum and Learning Materials
  • Draft PLAR process
  • Present 1 full day FNHMA pre-conference workshop (Vancouver, November 3, 2026)

Pilot and Evaluation

Key activities:

  • Piloting the curriculum and learning materials.

5. DELIVERABLES

The deliverables for this project are:

  • Core Competencies
  • Validate Core Competencies
  • Draft Curriculum and Learning Materials
  • Draft PLAR process
  • Present Competencies and Draft Curriculum at Pre-Conference
  • Pilot

6. REPORTING

The contractor will report to the Manager of Programs of FNHMA.

The contractor is required to provide a written report to FNHMA on a monthly basis on progress to date on the deliverables and to meet with FNHMA staff responsible for this project as required.

7. REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL

FNHMA is inviting proposals from Indigenous companies to provide the above goals and deliverables to FNHMA in the time frame specified.

As a contractor, you are being asked to provide one electronic copy of your proposal by May 18, 2026 at 5 p.m. EDT.

Andrea Aiabens
Manager of Programs
211 Akwesasne International Road
Akwesasne
ON K6H 0G5
andrea.aiabens@fnhma.ca

8. INQUIRES

Inquires, interpretations, and questions regarding the RFP are to be directed to Andrea Aiabens at andrea.aiabens@fnhma.ca

9. CLOSING DATE

RFP Closing Date: Monday May 18, 2026 at 5 PM EDT

10. REVIEW PROCESS AND SELECTION CRITERIA

Upon closing of the competition, the proposals will be reviewed internally by the Partner Organizations to make a recommendation for contract awards. Late proposals will not be accepted, and incomplete proposals will not be reviewed. Eligible proposals will be further reviewed according to the criteria appended to this RFP.

  1. Qualifications, expertise and experience of respondents.
  2. Experience working with Indigenous People organizations and Indigenous Communities.
  3. Implementation Plan and Approach.
  4. Innovative or creative approaches.
  5. Completeness and accuracy of the proposal format and supporting documentation.
  6. Cost and timeline for submission.

FNHMA expects to contract with the Indigenous firm whose proposal demonstrates that its performance of the work would be most advantageous to FNHMA, with price and other factors considered. FNHMA reserves the right to award a contract to other than the low contractor or not to make an award if that is deemed to serve its best interest.

FNHMA shall award the contract on May 29, 2026.

11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

FNHMA will have complete copyrights to all work completed. The Contractor will not retain copyright, title, or interest associated with the new content, etc. provided, developed by or for the Contractor for this project.

12. REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL SUBMISSION

COVER LETTER
A cover letter dated and signed by the contractor, who is authorized to negotiate and make commitments and provide any clarifications with respect to the proposal on behalf of the supplier of this contract.

PROFILE
Submit a brief profile indicating qualifications, expertise and years of experience and contact information. Include details regarding recent related experience over the past three years as well as other related experience in providing similar types of services.

To validate your experience, provide 2 references (including a current contact name and telephone number).

IMPLEMENTATION PLAN AND APPROACH
Provide a plan that clearly details the approach to carry out the work listed including timelines and any additional considerations. This can include innovative or creative approaches.

COSTS
Provide a budget and timeline for completion of the deliverables. Please include a budget for each of the major activities.

FNHMA contemplates a contract for a fixed fee, comprising the consultant’s charges for all work undertaken. Administrative costs such as conference calls or mailing can be charged in addition to the fees but must be discussed with the project authority ahead of time. Pre-approved travel costs, if any, are also in addition to the fees.
