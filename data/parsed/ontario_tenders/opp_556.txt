Detailed Description
This RFP is issued for the purpose of obtaining Proposals for Infant Formula, Nutritional Supplements and related Accessories for the Purchasers.
Proponents are to note that the Goods and related services contemplated in this RFP are critical to the participating Purchasers; hence the Proponents are required to clearly convey their approach to maintaining adequate and contingency supply in the event of any disruptions, labour or other.
This RFP also aims to provide the flexibility required for Purchasers to transition toward Baby-Friendly Initiative (BFI) designation, should they choose to do so during the life of the resulting agreement.
