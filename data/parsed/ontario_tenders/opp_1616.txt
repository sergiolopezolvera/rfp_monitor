Opportunity Details
Detailed Description
The purpose of this RFB, is for the Ministry to acquire the services of one (1) supplier for the provision of labour, materials, and equipment required to ensure proper performance of six groups of essential systems equipment, for the Ministry’s laboratory complex at 125 Resources Rd, Etobicoke, Ontario, by conducting regular planned / preventative maintenance routines, and providing emergency repair services.
The following equipment types, and their associated peripherals will be serviced: compressors, vacuum pumps, air handling units, cooling towers, walk-in freezers and refrigerators, chillers, fume / vent hoods, exhaust fans, and heat pumps.
Scope of Work
The qualified vendor will be required to perform preventative maintenance routines at varying intervals (monthly, quarterly, etc.), and provide emergency repairs as needed, on the essential equipment groups, at the MECP laboratory at 125 Resources Rd.

Detailed Description (French)
Cette demande de soumissions vise à trouver un (1) fournisseur qui devra fournir la main-d’œuvre, les matériaux et l’équipement nécessaires pour contrôler le rendement de six groupes d’équipement de systèmes, au complexe de laboratoires du ministère, situé au 125 Resources Road, à Etobicoke (Ontario).
