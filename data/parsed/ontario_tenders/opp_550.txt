Description:
RFB to provide Winter Jackets for Team Ontario, for the 2027 Canada Winter Games.
Each province/territory is represented by their athletes, coaches, managers, and mission staff wearing their winter jackets during travel, around the Athletes Village, at opening and closing ceremonies, medal ceremonies and other events while attending the 2027 Canada Winter Games in Quebec City. The Games occur from February 27 – March 14, 2027. Approximately 430 winter jackets will be required to be shipped to an address provided by the Ministry by January, 2027.
