Detailed Description
The Ministry is seeking a Vendor for the provision of Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning (“HVAC”) preventative maintenance services.
The Vendor shall provide comprehensive preventative maintenance services for the Ministry’s HVAC Systems as outlined in the Request For Bids (RFB) documents.
Scope of Work
All work performed must be in compliance with the standards, terms, conditions and specifications recommended by the Equipment’s Original Equipment Manufacturer (“OEM”), and any regulatory bodies having jurisdiction over the preventative maintenance of HVAC systems in Ontario.
