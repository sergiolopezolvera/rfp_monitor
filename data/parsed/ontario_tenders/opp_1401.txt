Detailed Description
OCWA is interested in retaining the services of up to five Bidders as “Vendors of Record” to perform reservoir, chamber and conduit repair services for its Facilities.
The successful Bidder(s) shall provide services to repairs may include the process equipment/piping/tanks within the reservoir, chamber or conduit and will exclude the amendments or changes to any of the original engineered drawings/designs at the South Peel Facilities for a three-year period, with an option of two years extension.
The closing date for this RFB is May 29, 2026.
Interested bidders must download the RFB document from the Ontario Tenders Portal:
https://ontariotenders.app.jaggaer.com.
All inquiries must be submitted through the Messages feature within the Jaggaer platform and addressed to Rashmi Vatish prior to the question deadline specified in the RFB document.
