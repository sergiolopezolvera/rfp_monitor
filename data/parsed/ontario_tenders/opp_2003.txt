Opportunity Details
Detailed Description
Request for Bids (RFB) for the provision of non‑scheduled air transportation by dedicated helicopter to complete lake sediment and water sampling in the Wawa Region, Province of Ontario.
Scope of Work
Lake sediment and water sampling for the Ontario Geological Survey (OGS) Branch of the Ontario Ministry of Energy and Mines will be drawn from various lakes in the Wawa Region, Province of Ontario.
Aircraft (Bell 206B), Flight Crew, and Ontario Public Service (OPS) employees will be based out of the Project Base Site at Wawa Airport, and the sampling locations will be within the Wawa, Lake Superior Park areas, in the Province of Ontario.
The Ministry will provide overall direction on the designated sampling sites and work to be undertaken during the project. There will be a requirement for the transportation of OPS personnel and/or equipment.
