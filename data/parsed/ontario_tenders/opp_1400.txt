Detailed Description
The Ministry is seeking a qualified Vendor(s) to supply and deliver handheld multifunction imagers and handheld thermal monoculars for use by the Ontario Provincial Police (OPP).
Scope of Work
The Vendor(s) shall supply and deliver Deliverables that meet the requirements outlined in the RFB and provide services that will efficiently facilitate the ordering, delivery and payment of these Deliverables.
The Deliverables required are set out in the following two (2) categories:
Category 1: Handheld Multifunction Imager
Category 2: Handheld Thermal Monocular
Bidders may submit a Bid for one (1) category or both categories of Deliverables.
Bidders must submit a separate Bid for each category in which they wish to Bid.
Each category of Deliverables will be evaluated and awarded separately.
