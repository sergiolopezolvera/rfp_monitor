Opportunity Details
Detailed Description
The Ontario Public Service (OPS) is asking suppliers to provide information on its Enterprise Project and Portfolio Management (EPPM) solution.
The EPPM solution RFI will inform OPS of technology solutions available for a standard solution. The solution in the RFI should foster on-going maturity by establishing a standard framework where successful project delivery is replicated across program areas. The solution would support management of the project methodology in the OPS.
Scope of Work
The Ontario Public Service (OPS) is asking suppliers to provide information on their Enterprise Project and Portfolio Management (EPPM) solution that would enable cross ministry and cross cluster/ITS project delivery.
The OPS is looking for the current state of the technology for this type of solution.
The services would be required within Ontario.
