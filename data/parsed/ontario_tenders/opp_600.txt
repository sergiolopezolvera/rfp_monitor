Detailed Description
Supply Ontario aims to deliver a comprehensive range of medical professional services to Ontario Public Service (OPS) and Broader Public Sector (BPS) entities, with the goal of supporting, managing, and providing care for Ontarians.
To accomplish this, Supply Ontario is soliciting the expertise of medical and health professionals across 11 distinct service categories to establish an enterprise vendor of record arrangement for the provision of medical services within seven service regions.
Scope of Work
Supply Ontario requires the services of medical/health professionals across 11 distinct service categories for Dentists and Dental Assistants, Registered Practical Nurses, Registered Nurses, Nurse Practitioners, Medical Doctors - Primary Care Practitioners, Psychiatrists, Pediatricians, Physiotherapists, Psychologists, Clinical Psychologists, and Personal Support Workers. These services are essential to support, manage, and provide care for Ontarians through Ontario Public Service (OPS) and Broader Public Sector (BPS) entities. The services will be required on an ongoing basis to ensure continuous and effective healthcare provision. They are needed across various OPS and BPS entities throughout Ontario.
Services to be provided through this vendor of record arrangement are intended to address the needs in correctional facilities, Provincial demonstration schools and various other healthcare spaces.

Detailed Description (French)
ApprovisiOntario vise à offrir une gamme complète de services professionnels médicaux aux entités de la fonction publique de l’Ontario (FPO) et du secteur parapublic dans le but de soutenir, de gérer et de fournir des soins aux Ontariennes et aux Ontariens.
Pour ce faire, ApprovisiOntario sollicite l’expertise de professionnels de la santé dans 11 catégories de services distinctes afin d’établir une entente de fournisseur officiel pour la prestation de services médicaux dans 7 régions de service.

Scope of Work (French)
Scope of Work (French)
ApprovisiOntario a besoin des services de professionnels de la santé dans 11 catégories de services distinctes pour les dentistes, les infirmiers auxiliaires autorisés, les infirmiers autorisés, les infirmiers praticiens, les médecins généralistes, les psychiatres, les pédiatres, les physiothérapeutes, les psychologues, les psychologues cliniciens et les préposés aux services de soutien à la personne.
