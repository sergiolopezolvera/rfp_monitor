Detailed Description
Under this Request for Bids the Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery and Procurement is procuring Cohesity backup and recovery services.
Scope of Work
Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery and Procurement uses backup and storage management software from Cohesity primarily in the Guelph Data Centre, designated third-party data centres, and remote hosts across the province.
Cohesity products (formerly Veritas) support the continuity of business operations through timely access and restoration of data, in the event of loss of data due to accidental deletion, corruption, system failure, or disaster.
