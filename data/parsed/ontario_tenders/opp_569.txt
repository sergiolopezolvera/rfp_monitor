Description:
The purpose of this Request for Bids (RFB) is to acquire a Vendor of Record for the rebuild of Marine Engines and Generators for Marine East.

The objective of this RFB is to establish a Vendor of Record for the overhaul of marine Engines and Generator’s. The selected vendor will be responsible for providing all necessary parts and labour to perform Engine and Generator rebuilds at intervals mandated by Transport Canada. These services will apply to all Engine and Generator within the Ministry of Transportation’s Marine East Division inventory.
