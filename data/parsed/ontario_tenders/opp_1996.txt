Detailed Description
Analyse sediment samples, for methyl mercury, and benthic invertebrate tissue samples, for total mercury and methyl mercury, collected from Great Lakes nearshore waters in 2026 and 2027.
Scope of Work
Analyse up to one hundred forty-one (141) sediment samples from Lake Ontario, St. Clair River and Thunder Bay in 2026.
Analyse up to twenty-nine (29) benthic invertebrate tissue samples from the St. Clair River for total and methyl mercury analysis in 2026.
Analyse up to two hundred eighty-six (286) sediment samples from Lake Erie, St. Lawrence River, and Peninsula Harbour for methyl mercury analysis in 2027.
Analyse up to twelve (12) tissue samples from Peninsula Harbour for analysis of total and methyl mercury in 2027.
After collection, all sediment and tissue samples will be frozen and stored at -18 degrees Celsius.
