Tender Notice - Central Regional Correctional Centre
DTI Project Number: Y17 BY477D
Tender Package TP.09 - Casework, Building Finishes, Misc. Specialties and Misc. Site Finishes
The Department of Transportation and Infrastructure (DTI) has contracted Bird Construction Group to act as the Construction Manager to complete the Central Regional Correctional Centre located in Minto, NB.
This is a request for proposal (RFP) to perform either of the following scopes of work;
- TP.09a - FINISH CARPENTRY AND CASEWORK
- TP.09b - FLOORING
- TP.09c - EPOXY FINISHING SYSTEMS
- TP.09d - PAINTING
- TP.09e - SIGNAGE
- TP.09f - MISC. SPECIALTIES (FOLDING PARTITIONS, LOCKERS, TOILET ACCESSORIES,
FLAGPOLES)
- TP.09g - MOBILE SHELVING/LAUNDRY SHELVING
- TP.09h - ROLLER WINDOW SHADES
- TP.09i - CURBS & WALKS
- TP.09j - SECURITY FENCING
- TP.09k - OVERHEAD DOORS & SHUTTERS
for the above-mentioned project and must be submitted directly to Bird Construction Group on behalf of The Department of Transportation and Infrastructure (DTI) as instructed in the RFP/Tender documents.
Closing Date: June 4, 2026 @ 2:00 PM (AST)
