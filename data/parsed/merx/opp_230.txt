AI Overview:
Seeking a Liquid Waste Removal Company to provide waste removal, debris cleanup, and water replenishment services for toilets and tanks at campsites and day use areas.

Description:
a) Project Background: the South West area of Parks South Region requires the services of a Liquid Waste Removal Company, to remove liquid waste and debris and to provide charging (water replenishment) services for toilets and other tanks within the area campsites and day use areas in consultation with the contracting manager or designate. b) Project Objective: The Province’s Contract Manager has the responsibility for ensuring that all charging, emptying and year end cleaning of the toilet tanks occurs in a timely and effective manner.
There are four major areas included in this RFP. A proponent may bid on all locations or any combination of locations. The evaluation will consider the best value for the Province in selecting the Proponent(s) by each location separately.
