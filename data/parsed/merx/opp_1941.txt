Description:
This is a contract offered for works on “Grading” for the BC Timber Sales Roads located in the North and South Zones of the Okanagan-Columbia Business Area. Grading works will be assigned on an “as needed” basis to improve timber sale access in accordance with the fiscal timber sales plan. Grading will also facilitate other planned engineering projects such as access for maintenance, construction, and deactivation. Works may be located anywhere in the North and South Zone operating areas.
