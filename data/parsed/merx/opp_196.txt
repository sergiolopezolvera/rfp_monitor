Description:
The proposed contract consists of silviculture implementation services, specifically, planting quality pay plots and prework field days for the 2026
spring
and summer
plant. Planting quality pay plots will be conducted on approximately
30 blocks
, totaling
631.6 ha
and requiring approximately
649 plots
from mid April to July 24, 2026. Spring planting will end by June 21, 2026, and summer planting should start by July 5, 2026.
Contractors must ensure they can meet reporting and deliverables deadlines during these periods. Quality will be assessed as per the FS704 method, and field data will be collected using Plant Wizard. The spring plant must be completed by June 21, 2026. The summer plant must be completed by July 26, 2026. Bidders must anticipate being present for field pre-work meetings with the planting contractors.
Planting units must be inspected, and results given within
2 days (this includes weekends)
of a request for inspection by the planting contractor. Plant Wizard must be used to collect field data. The contractor is not required to generate payment certificates, however, must provide data and plot location map (i.e., sync) within 2 days of a request for inspection.
