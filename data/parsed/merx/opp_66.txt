Comprehensive helicopter landing area management services including construction of new helipads, inspection and repair of existing sites, timber falling, and danger tree assessment with strict emergency and standard response times.

Description:
The Contractor shall provide comprehensive access management for helicopter landing areas and helipads, including the construction of new helipads to specified standards, as well as the inspection and repair of existing helicopter access sites by qualified personnel. Additionally, the scope of work includes, when required, the falling and clearing of timber, maintaining existing helicopter access sites, along with the assessment and removal of windfall and danger trees to ensure site safety and compliance. Danger Tree Assessment and Falling along Forest Service Roads and worksites forms an integral part of this contract.
Response Time
To ensure operational continuity and safety, the contractor is contractually mandated to adhere to strict response times, categorized by the severity of the request. For emergency situations that threaten life, property, or the environment, a rapid response must occur within 48 hours to mitigate immediate danger and initiate restoration services. Conversely, for standard, non-urgent work requests, the contractor must provide a response or arrive onsite within 7 calendar days.
