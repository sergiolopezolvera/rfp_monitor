Description of the Requirement:
Indigenous Services Canada has a requirement for ORACLE/MS SQL Server Database Administration And Support Services on an as and when required basis via Task Authorizations (TA) under the TBIPS Supply Arrangement (SA) method of supply. It is intended to result in the award of 1 contract for two (2) years plus three (3) one-year irrevocable options allowing Canada to extend the term of the contract.
Security:
There are security requirements associated with this requirement. Please note that SA Holders do not need to have the required clearance to submit a bid.
