Metrolinx is accepting Submissions for Request for Information for PRESTO Device Refresh.
Metrolinx/Payments (PRESTO) is preparing to re-procure approximately 20,000 fare payment
and sales devices across 11 transit agencies in the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area (GTHA)
and Ottawa. Metrolinx/PRESTO recently awarded five new contracts for its back-office,
integration, and digital layers (Automated Fare Collection System (AFCS), System Integrator
Services (SIS), Service Integration and Management (SIAM), contact center, and digital
channels). The device refresh is a subsequent, separate procurement program that interfaces
with these contracts. Device vendors will be required to integrate with the new AFCS via
published REST APIs and PRESTO’s payment acquirer. Additionally, device vendors will need
to work with PRESTO’s System Integrator (SIS) and Service Integration and Management
(SIAM) vendor.
