Description:
Parkland County (the “County”)) is seeking responses from experienced service professionals capable of providing Health and Safety Training Services to County staff. Through this opportunity, the County intends to prequalify several service professionals, who will then enter into a Master Framework Agreement and can subsequently be engaged to provide services, upon request, over a three year term. Respondents must be capable of providing Health and Safety Training Services in one or more of the following four service categories:
1. First Aid Certifications;
2. Safe Equipment Use;
3. General Safety; and
4. Animal Safety.
