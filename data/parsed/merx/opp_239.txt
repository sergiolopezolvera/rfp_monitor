Description:
This opportunity is for the mechanical site preparation of two hundred and ninety-seven point five hectares (297.5 ha) of harvested openings to help BC Timber Sales meet legal reforestation milestone obligations. Works will include mounding and patch scarification with a tracked excavator to create between fourteen hundred (1,400) and sixteen hundred (1,600) plantable spots per hectare. Ten (10) hours of access and road works will be included for unforeseen road rehabilitation or deactivation that is authorized by the BC Timber Sales representative.
