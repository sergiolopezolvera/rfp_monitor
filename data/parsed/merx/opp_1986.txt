COMPETITION ANBL 024 - 27
REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS -
CONVENIENCE AGENT CHANNEL -
HAMPTON, NB
The Negotiated Request for Proposals (NRFP) is an invitation by ANBL to interested parties to submit proposals to act as a Tier 1 agent of ANBL under the Convenience Agent Channel in Hampton, NB. Submissions are invited and shall be received up to 1:30 pm on June 5, 2026.
RFP documents may be obtained from Bonfire at https://anbl.bonfirehub.ca/opportunities
There will be a question period from time of posting until 4:30 pm on May 19,2026 when all questions regarding the RFP can be addressed through Bonfire. No inquiries will be accepted after this date.
ANBL will respond to all inquiries by May 19,2026 by issuing an addendum that will be posted on Bonfire.
This is a tender notice only. In order to submit a bid, you must obtain official tender documents from the New Brunswick Opportunities Network, another authorized tendering service or as indicated in the tender notice.
