Description:
ATB Financial is seeking to establish a group of pre-qualified Vendors to provide design, installation, and specialized operational support for meeting room and event space technologies. This group will support ATB’s Client Experience & Technology (CXT) team in delivering consistent technology standards across corporate headquarters and 150+ branch/agency locations. The goal is to streamline procurement through a master agreement, allowing ATB to execute work via Simplified RFPs (S-RFPs) for specific renovations, new builds, or complex maintenance. Through this Request for Proposal (RFP), we aim to identify Proponent(s) capable of delivering based on strict timelines, competitive pricing, exceptional client service and satisfaction, and value-added services while remaining adaptive to our ever-changing needs.
