Contact name: Caroline Sirois

Contact email: (613) 294-3519

Contact phone: caroline.sirois@nrcan-rncan.gc.ca

Description:
Acquisition and classification of airborne topographic lidar data for multiple regions of Labrador, including Nain, Postville, Hopedale, Makkovik and Natuashish. Optional service areas also include communities of Cartwright, Black Tickle, Rigolet, Charlottetown, Port Hope Simpson, Mary’s Harbour-St. Lewis, Happy Valley-Goose Bay and Churchill Falls.
