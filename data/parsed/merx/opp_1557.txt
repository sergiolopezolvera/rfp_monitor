Description:
British Columbia Hydro and Power Authority (“BC Hydro”) invites interested parties to submit responses to this RFSQ (each, a “Response”) to qualify for a potential award of a master services agreement (“MSA”) for Market Research Services
The general scope of the Services includes, but is not limited to, undertaking end-to-end research studies that include some or all of the following elements: strategy consultation, project management, questionnaire design, data collection, analysis through to reporting and evaluation, and other market research services. The categories of the Services are as follows:
Category 1: Quantitative Analytical Research Services
Category 2: Qualitative Analytical Research Service
Category 3: Public Engagement and Consultation Services
Category 4: Other Research Services
Respondents may submit a response to one or more of the categories listed above.
