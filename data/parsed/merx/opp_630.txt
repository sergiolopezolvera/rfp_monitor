Description:
This RFP is being issued by Provincial Health Services Authority (“PHSA”) on behalf of BC Renal (“BCR”), a provincial agency that plans and coordinates kidney (renal) care for patients across British Columbia. As part of the PHSA, BC Renal works with the regional Health Authority kidney programs and other partners to deliver services like hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, and chronic kidney disease clinics throughout the province. The objective is to identify a qualified Proponent (or Proponents) to enter into a non-exclusive Contract (or Contracts) between a Proponent and the PHSA, for the supply of the Community Pharmacy Services
