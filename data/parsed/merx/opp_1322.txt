Description:
Notice is hereby given that the Village of Lions Bay is contemplating making a direct award of a contract to Waste Control Services Inc. (the “Supplier”).
The high-level details of the contemplated contract are generally described below.
Provision of:
Two weekly curbside landfill garbage pickup
Weekly curbside compostables/yard waste pickup
Weekly separated recycling pickup
The term of the contract is expected to be 5 years.
The aggregate value of the contract is expected not to exceed, exclusive of applicable taxes $1,100,000.
