Description:
This Request for Supplier Qualifications (“RFSQ”) is an invitation by Parkland County (the “County”) to prospective respondents to qualify for eligibility to provide support for land-based acquisitions, valuations, agreements, negotiations, consultations, management (Alberta Energy Regulator (AER) Regulated Surface Rights), and other related activities
\
The County intends to pre-qualify Consultants to be included on a Master Roster List for a period of three years with no options to extend. Parkland County will select up to 5 respondents to be included on the pre-qualified list.
