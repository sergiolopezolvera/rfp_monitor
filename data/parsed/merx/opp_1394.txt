All required supporting documentation and bid submission must be submitted on 2026-05-15 by 14h00 EDT. All bids submitted after 14h00 EDT will result in the bid being declared non-responsive. Department of National Defence (DND) has a requirement for four (4) vehicle mechanic technicians to assist in the support and maintenance of Tactical Armoured Patrol Vehicle (TAPV) fleet vehicles at Garrison Petawawa. The technicians will be required at Garrison Petawawa where tasks may include, but are not limited to, conducting inspections, diagnosis, and repairs of TAPV fleet vehicles and their equipment.
