Description:
The OAG is undertaking an initiative to modernize its information management framework, policies, and practices to ensure alignment with evolving technologies, applicable legislation, and operational needs. As part of this effort, the OAG seeks independent advisory support to assess contemporary approaches to managing organizational information and to provide strategic guidance that will support informed decision-making regarding future tools, governance models, and practices.
