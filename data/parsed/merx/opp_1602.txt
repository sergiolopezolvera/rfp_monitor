The Minister of Public Works and Government Services Canada (PWGSC) (also known as Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC)) on behalf of the Receiver General for Canada (RG) has a requirement for a Cash Order Service (COS) to enable participating Federal Organizations to access large values of currency and bank drafts to support key operational requirements, as described in the Statement of Work.
The period of the resulting Contract is four years from date of Contract Award. Any resulting contract will include an irrevocable option to extend the resulting contract term by up to one additional one-year period, as well as a four-month transition period, under the same conditions.
There are no security requirements associated with this solicitation of offers.
The requirement is solely limited to Canadian services.
