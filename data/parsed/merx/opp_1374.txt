Description:
The Town of Banff (the Town) is seeking proposals from qualified proponents to conduct an operational review of the Fenlands Banff Recreation Centre. The Town is seeking an operational review of the Fenlands Banff Recreation Centre to assess current service delivery, utilization, staffing, cost recovery, as well as the operational implications of planned facility enhancements, with the goal of identifying practical recommendations that support long-term sustainability and community value.
