Seeking proposals for senior strategic advisory services in the Transportation and Logistics sector to support small and medium-sized enterprises in Alberta facing industry disruptions.

Description:
Mount Royal University (MRU) is seeking proposals from qualified proponents with expertise in the Transportation and Logistics (T&L) sector to provide senior strategic advisory services in support of initiatives for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in Alberta, addressing sector disruptions such as labour shortages, tariffs, supply chain volatility, and skills gaps. The Successful Proponent will work with MRU to lead the design and delivery of programs and initiatives, establish relationships with partners and contractors, and support the achievement of defined performance metrics, including participation and program outcomes.
