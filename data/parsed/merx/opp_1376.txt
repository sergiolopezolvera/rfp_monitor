Description:
Notice is hereby given by the Vancouver Public Library Board (“VPL”) of its intent to contract with Cascadia Partners for the provision of consulting services.
Cascadia Partners will provide consulting services to VPL to align Strategic Planning, Onboarding and Core Staff Training consulting projects, taking place from May 11th, 2026 to June 30th, 2027. Cascadia Partners is currently working with VPL on the three projects. The contract is valued at approximately $25,000.
