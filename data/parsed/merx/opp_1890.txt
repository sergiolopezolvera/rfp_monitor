Description:
Halifax Water is seeking qualified suppliers for the moving and relocation of water supply plant sludge at the J.D. Kline Water Supply Plant, 1749, Pockwock Road, Upper Hammonds Plains. Contractor will need an 85’ reach excavator to collect sludge from Primary drying beds, and dump trucks with seals to move to secondary drying beds 18” to 24” in size. All activity is done on-site.
