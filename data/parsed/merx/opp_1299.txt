Description:
The City of Parksville is seeking qualified proponents to design, supply, and install decorative outdoor lighting within the downtown core. This project forms a key component of the City’s Downtown Revitalization Strategy, which aims to create an attractive, pedestrian-friendly, and economically vibrant downtown that reflects Parksville’s unique coastal identity. The City is looking for innovative yet practical lighting solutions that enhance nighttime character, support year-round activation of public spaces, and contribute to a safe and welcoming environment for residents, businesses, and visitors. Proposed lighting systems must follow DarkSky friendly principles, be durable and resistant to vandalism, and demonstrate proven performance in other municipal or public settings.
