REAL ESTATE BROKERAGE SERVICES
REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
# 26-030
CITY OF MIRAMICHI
OPERATION OF SEASONAL CANTEEN / RETAURANT AT MIDDLE ISLAND HISTORICAL PARK CENTER 2026 - 2030
The City of Miramichi invites qualified operators to submit proposals for the operation of a seasonal canteen/restaurant at Middle Island Historical Park. The City wishes to enter into a rental contract with operators for a period of up to five (5) years and the City of Miramichi expects the business to be open from June 1st to October 1st each year of the contract.
The Request For Proposal Package must be obtained through the online portal at https://miramichi.bidsandtenders.ca
Proposals will be received by the undersigned until 11:00 a.m., May 5th , 2026.
The City of Miramichi reserves the right to accept or reject any or all proposals.
Rhonda Ripley
City Clerk
