Description:
This Request for Proposals (the “RFP”) is an invitation by Build Nova Scotia (“BNS”) to prospective proponents to submit proposals for BNS-2627-0019 Former Coalburn Mine Site Phase I/II ESA. BNS is seeking the services of a consultant with experienced and qualified site professionals, senior environmental experts, and field personnel to conduct an environmental investigation at the former Coalburn coal mine site, in order to provide a Phase I/II ESA report and a Class D remediation estimate for the Crown Land Property.
