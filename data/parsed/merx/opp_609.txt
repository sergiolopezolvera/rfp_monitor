Description:
SAIT is inviting experienced and qualified suppliers of Project Management Consulting Services to submit proposals outlining their approach to conducting a market analysis for a vertiport feasibility study. This RFP seeks a solution that evaluates the commercial, strategic, and operational viability of establishing a vertiport in the Calgary region of Alberta. The scope of work includes site selection analysis within the region; assessment of market demand for Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS) and Electric Vertical Take-Off and Landing (eVTOL) operations; competitive and benchmarking studies; development of partnership and ecosystem strategies; and identification of potential revenue streams. Experience with regulatory frameworks and airport operations is preferred. The scope also includes concept design and the preparation of renderings.
