Description:
The Capital Regional District (CRD) is seeking proposals for an experienced proponent to develop a Regional Parks Stewardship Plan and lead agency/interest-holder engagement on the draft plan concepts. The Stewardship Plan will establish a comprehensive, systemwide policy
framework that brings together conservation, recreation (visitor experience), and First Nations cultural and traditional values to guide the long-term stewardship of CRD regional parks.
