Description of Work:
The IM/IT Division of the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions has a requirement for specialized of strategic information governance professional services, on an as and when requested basis, to support OSFI’s Enterprise Information Management (EIM) team in a series of projects required to support ongoing planning and management of its corporate EIM projects, over the next two or more years.
