Metrolinx is accepting Submissions for
Request for Information for Operations and Safety Digital Transformation Roadmap.
Metrolinx has developed a 12-year Operations and Safety Digital Transformation Roadmap (the “Roadmap”) to modernize the technology ecosystem that supports day-to-day transit operations, safety-critical functions, and enabling capabilities. The Roadmap responds to increasing service demands driven by GO Expansion and establishes a staged approach to delivery and sustainment over multiple years. Metrolinx is now progressing into broader planning and implementation efforts and is seeking market input to help shape an effective procurement and delivery approach.
