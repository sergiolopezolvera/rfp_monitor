NOTICE OF PROPOSED PROCUREMENT M7594-262854 – Technical Third Part Expert Assistance (T3PEA) 1. This Notice of Proposed Procurement (NPP) is issued pursuant to PWGSC Supply Arrangement (SA) EN578-150229 and covers a requirement under Streams 1-4.
2. Only Bidders holding a Supply Arrangement Agreement for all 4 streams are allowed access and download a copy of the solicitations documents and are allowed to submit a bid.
3. The Contractor is required to provide to Canada, on behalf of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, On-Site Technical Inspection, Technical Review, Technical Advice, and Risk Advice in accordance with the Statement of Work.
4. Term of Contract : The period of the Contract is from date of Contract to December 31, 2028 inclusive.
5. There are no security requirements associated with this requirement.
6. This NPP covers a requirement solicited amongst Supply Arrangement holders. In order for new suppliers to bid on this solicitation, the new supplier must first qualify for a Supply Arrangement. The process to qualify for a SA is detailed in RFSA # EN578-150229 and can be found on CanadaBuys (https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/tender-notice/pw-nss-006-28191). Canada is not required to delay the award of a resulting contract pending the evaluation of an arrangement and issuance of a SA by the PWGSC Supply Arrangement Authority.
