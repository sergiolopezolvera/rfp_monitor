Description:
The City of Calgary (“the City”), on behalf of the Calgary Police Service (“CPS”), is seeking a qualified Supplier capable of delivering, implementing, maintaining, and supporting a comprehensive Automated Traffic Enforcement Program. The proposed solution must include either a cloud-based or on premise software platform, as well as all required physical infrastructure to support Mobile Photo Radar (speed) equipment and Intersection Safety Devices capable of red light and speed enforcement.
