Description:
The Regional District of Okanagan‑Similkameen (RDOS) is delivering the HomeEnergy Guide Program, a homeowner‑focused initiative designed to support residents in planning and completing energy efficiency retrofits. The program provides neutral, non‑commercial guidance to help homeowners understand their home’s energy performance, identify cost‑effective upgrade pathways, and access available rebates and incentives. The HomeEnergy Guide Program is aligned with RDOS climate and energy objectives, including improving housing comfort and resilience, reducing household energy use and emissions, and supporting informed renovation decisions through a whole‑home, house‑as‑a‑system approach. Central to the program is the use of standardized home energy evaluations to establish a performance baseline and confirm post‑retrofit improvements. RDOS is seeking Expressions of Interest from qualified energy assessors to support current and anticipated demand for home energy evaluations across the Okanagan‑Similkameen region.
