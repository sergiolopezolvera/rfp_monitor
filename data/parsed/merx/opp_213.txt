Description:
ENMAX Corporation and its owned affiliates require management consulting services. The Consultant shall provide management consulting services for strategic, human resources, financial, and operational advisory support across the full engagement lifecycle, from diagnostic assessment through implementation and benefits realization. The Consultant’s shall provide advisory and delivery support across prioritized business initiatives, within the context of operating in the Alberta utility environment. Consultants should have the capacity to self-perform and/ or facilitate management consulting services for the following service categories.
Strategic Consulting • Human Resource Consulting • Financial Consulting • Operational Consulting
