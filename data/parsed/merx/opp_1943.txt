AI Overview:
BC Hydro seeks independent review and validation services for project cost estimates, including methodology review, validation of cost components, contingency assessment, benchmarking, and preparation of review reports.

Description:
This Notice to Vendors 24399 provides information in advance of the RFP to assist potential proponents in understanding BC Hydro’s requirements and to better prepare for responding to the RFP.
The scope of services will generally include independent review and validation of project cost estimates, including, but not limited to:
Review of estimate methodology, assumptions, and completeness
Validation of key cost components and construction approach
Assessment of contingency, risks, and schedule alignment
High-level benchmarking and market reasonableness
Preparation of concise review reports with findings and recommendations
On an as-required basis and subject to Work Order authorization, to perform other related services, such as development of estimates upon request
