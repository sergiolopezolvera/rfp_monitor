Title: WS5676820514 - Wellington Wall Masonry Maintenance – Piers 34 to 63

Status: This solicitation is OPEN

Reference Number: WS5676820514-Doc5678614180

Solicitation Number: WS5676820514

Issuing Organization: Department of Public Works and Government Services (PSPC)

Location: National Capital Region

Publication date: 2026-05-05

Closing date: 2026-05-21

Contact name: Bullard, Jonathan (SPAC/PSPC) (il-lui / he-him)

Contact email: jonathan.bullard@tpsgc-pwgsc.gc.ca

Description:
The project consists of executing a comprehensive conservation program that arrests the active deterioration of the 19th-century Wellington Wall masonry and ironwork, thereby ensuring the structure's long-term stability and preservation of its heritage value. This is a Request for Proposal only for Holders of Supply Arrangement (SA) EP938-232165. Select General Contractor SA holders have been invited to participate per the terms and conditions set out in the SA. If other bidders are interested in submitting a proposal they will have to qualify for the Supply Arrangement before they are given an opportunity to bid on this requirement. If your company is interested in becoming a qualified supplier on the Supply Arrangement, you can find the full solicitation documents using this link. https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/tender-notice/ws4128712580-doc4128852077
