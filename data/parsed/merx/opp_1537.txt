Contact name: Dwyer, Alexandria (SPAC/PSPC) (elle-la / she-her)

Contact email: alexandria.dwyer@tpsgc-pwgsc.gc.ca

Description:
Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC) is seeking a consultant team to complete repair designs and construction tender documentation to address the deteriorated floor structure of the lantern room at the Cape Race Lighthouse. The repair design scope will involve replacement of the deteriorated floor structure of the lantern room, conservation repairs to the lantern enclosure assembly, and mechanical and electrical considerations in support of the main repairs. The list of the pre-qualified firms invited that qualified under the Architecture & interior Design Supply Arrangement E0225-152290 in no particular order are as follows: 1. DSRA Architecture Inc. 2. MacKay-Lyons Sweetapple Architects 3. DFS Inc architecture & design 4. Lat49 Architecture Inc. 5. Architecture49 Inc. 6. Fowler Bauld & Mitchell Ltd. 7. EastPoint Architecture Partners 8. Hatch Architecture Ltd. 9. Republic Architecture Inc. 10. Gibbons Snow Architects Inc. 11. Stantec Architecture Ltd. 12. Fougere Menchenton Architecture Inc. Reference the solicitation for further details.
