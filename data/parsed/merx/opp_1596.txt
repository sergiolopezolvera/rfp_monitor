Description:
The Town of Mahone Bay is seeking proposals from qualified proponents to undertake a hydrological and hydraulic assessment of a defined study area along Ernst Brook, located in Mahone Bay, NS. Stage 1 (required report) will present study data and initial options, Stage 2 (optional report to be requested on completion of Stage 1) will consider options and develop pre-design for Jubilee Park property.
