Description:
This project is the inspection, identification, and documentation of Calgary Transit’s underground cable infrastructure for downtown Calgary in an underground vaults/duct bank system. This scope includes cable tracing, labelling, condition verification, and record validation. The collected field data will be compiled into accurate drawings, cable schedules, and inspection reports.
