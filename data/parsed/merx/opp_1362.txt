Description of the Requirement:
The Department of National Defence (DND) has a requirement for two (2) Intermediate Software Developers and one (1) Intermediate Project Leader to support the development, scaling, and sustainment of the cloud-based Innovation application, Ignitor. Ignitor will be created and sustained to advance innovation and culture evolution initiatives under the Royal Canadian Air Force (RCAF)’s innovation team, Plan Qulliq.
