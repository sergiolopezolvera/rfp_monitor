Description:
The City of Terrace (CoT) is seeking proposals from qualified consulting firms to provide project management services for the planning and delivery of a multimillion-dollar water supply system for the Skeena Industrial Development Park (SIDP). The selected consultant team will lead the initiation phase, focusing on establishing the foundation necessary to support future phases, funding readiness, and informed decision-making.
