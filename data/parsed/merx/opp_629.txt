Seeking mechanical site preparation services for 185.2 hectares of harvested openings, including mounding and patch scarification to create 1,400-1,600 plantable spots per hectare.

Description:
This opportunity is for the mechanical site preparation of one hundred and eighty-five point two hectares (185.2 ha) of harvested openings to help BC Timber Sales meet legal reforestation milestone obligations. Works will include mounding and patch scarification with a tracked excavator to create fourteen to sixteen hundred (1,400 - 1,600) plantable spots per hectare.
Ten (10) hours of access and road works will be included for unforeseen road rehabilitation or deactivation that is authorized by the BC Timber Sales representative.
