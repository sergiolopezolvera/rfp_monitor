Description:
Please note that late submissions will not be considered or accepted. The Request for Standing Offer seeks the provision of vehicle body repair and refinishing services on an as-and-when requested basis for vehicles under the responsibility of Department of National Defence, Garrison Petawawa and Lodger Units. There are 2 streams applicable to this solicitation: Stream 1, Ontarion Region (excluding NCR) and Stream 2, Procurement Strategy for Indigenous Business (PSIB). Canada may award up to 2 standing offers. The period of work covers services requested from the date of issuance to 31 May 2029. Offerors should submit all questions in writing no later than 22 May 2026. Canada may not respond to questions submitted after this.
