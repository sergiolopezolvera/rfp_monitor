Description:
The Town of Trochu is seeking proposals for Solid Waste Management Services through an open Request for Proposal (RFP). The scope includes the collection and transfer of residential and commercial waste to the Trochu Transfer Site, with options for organic waste collection. The RFP encourages the inclusion of alternative waste conversion technologies and allows for single or multiple stream contracts.
