Description:
The proposed contract requires the provision of approximately 1479 hectares of Free Growing Silviculture Surveys located across the Morice and Bulkley operating areas in the Babine Business Area.
The contract will require a range of qualified professionals and technical staff working in various conditions including isolated areas.
Contract deliverables will be planned for completion throughout the fiscal year to meet the business area quarterly targets. Projects will be completed concurrently as per the approved work progress plan and schedules.
