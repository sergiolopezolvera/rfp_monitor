Description:
This bid solicitation cancels and supersedes previous bid solicitation number ET022-261291 - Doc5536920380 dated 2026-02-17 with a closing of 2026-03-20 at 2pm CST. A debriefing or feedback session will be provided upon request to bidders/offerors/suppliers who bid on the previous solicitation. Public Works and Government Services Canada (PWGSC(PSPC)), on behalf of Parks Canada (PC) is seeking to retain a Contractor to provide construction services associated with the decommissioning and replacement of a fuel storage tank system at the maintenance compound of Batoche National Historic Site in Saskatchewan. There will be an optional virtual Bidders' conference held via MS Teams on Tuesday, May 26, 2026 at 1 PM local time (CST, Saskatchewan). To attend the Bidders' must communicate with the Contracting Authority no later than Friday, May 22, 2026 at 4 PM CST to confirm attendance and provide the name(s) of their representatives.
