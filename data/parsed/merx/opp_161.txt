Description:
The Courts Administration Service (CAS) has a requirement for one Senior Procurement Specialist to support the Procurement Section in advancing departmental modernization initiatives and in managing medium-complexity procurement files as well as high-complexity special project requirements.
PLEASE NOTE: This requirement is a voluntary set-aside under PSIB and is open only to those Indigenous Supply Arrangement Holders under E60ZT-18TSPS who qualified under Tier 1, under the National Capital Region, level of expertise and for the following category: 3.7 PROCUREMENT SPECIALIST- Level 3 (senior)
