Description:
The Municipality of the County of Victoria invites proposals from qualified consultants to conduct a comprehensive performance evaluation of the Chief Administrative Officer (CAO). The procurement is being administered on behalf of the Municipality in accordance with its policies. The successful proponent will also develop and recommend a formal, structured performance appraisal process and supporting tools for ongoing use by Council in future evaluation cycles.
