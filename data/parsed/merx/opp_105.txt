The purpose for renting external generators is to provide reliable back up power for the Network Operations Control Centre (NOCC) facility in the event of a utility power interruption. The backup generators will support the continuity of critical operational systems housed within the NOCC facility.
This request to Tender is for the rental of two (2) temporary backup generators configured for parallel operation, inclusive of installation, commissioning, ongoing operational support, maintenance and end-of-term removal at the Network Operations Control Centre (NOCC) facility.
