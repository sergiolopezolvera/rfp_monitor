Seeking a service provider for environmentally responsible collection and disposal of chemical waste from water facilities.

Description:
Halifax Water Facilities collect chemical waste from spills, expired and contaminated product, lab reagents etc., and require environmentally responsible collection and disposal. Chemical waste could be partially filled drums of a corrosive substance, jerry cans filled with lab reagent waste containing cyanide, rags, spill pads or pails collected from chemical spills, etc. We are seeking company to pick up, remove and dispose of chemical waste products from Halifax Water Facilities throughout HRM.
