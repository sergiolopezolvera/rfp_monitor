Red Deer County (the “County”) invites qualified Commercial Real Estate Brokerage firms (“Proponents”) to submit an Expression of Interest and proposal to provide marketing and sales services for County-owned industrial/commercial land at the Junction 42 Business Campus (“Junction 42”).
