Description:
The Board of Trustees of Wolf Creek School Division is considering a partnership that can support Success For All Students. This RFQ is not a tender call. The RFQ is intended to allow the Board to evaluate the qualifications of companies or individuals (hereafter called the Bidder) to provide the required services. The Board will identify the successful Bidder, if any, and then undertake subsequent discussions and negotiations respecting fundamentals of the proposal. At the sole discretion of the Board, a School-Based Rehabilitation Services Contract can be implemented with their selected Bidder should the Board decide that best meets their requirements both currently and in the future.
