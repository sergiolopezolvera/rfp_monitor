Description:
Canada is seeking bids to provide support, as the rapid expansion and the high and sustained level of operational activities undertaken by the Canadian Armed Forces (CAF) have resulted in a shortage of spaces for the initial trade training of CAF vehicle technicians. For this reason, the 2nd Canadian Division is seeking contractor support to deliver initial vehicle technician training for the Department of National Defence.
