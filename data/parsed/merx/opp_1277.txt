Description:
Notice is hereby given that the Capital Regional District (the “CRD”), intends to directly award funds to an existing Contract to the following supplier to include studies associated with the Island Corridor (IC):
Access Planning Ltd.
1250 Homer Street,
Vancouver, BC
V6B 1C6
The supplier competed in October, 2025 under RFP #2025-RPT-008 CRD ‘Regional Transportation Plan’, and was identified as the winning Proponent. The work associated with the Island Corridor is directly related to the existing work already underway in this contract surrounding CRD’s Regional Transportation Planning initiative. The value associated with the additional scope is anticipated to be $172,000 with an associated contingency allowance bringing the total up to $200,000.
.
In accordance with applicable trade agreements, CRD has chosen not to proceed with a competitive process for the following reason:
• the goods or services can be supplied only by the selected supplier and no alternative or substitute exists for the following reason:
(a) due to an absence of competition for technical reasons.
Vendors who wish to challenge this decision should submit a detailed written objection including documentation demonstrating that they meet the requirements set out above. This is to be addressed for the attention of Kara Mullin, by email before 2:00pm Pacific Time, May 11th, 2026. CRD
