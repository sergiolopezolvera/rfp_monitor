Description:
This Notice of Proposed Procurement ('NPP') is issued to notify the supplier community that the Province of Nova Scotia (the 'Province'), on behalf of Nova Scotia Health ('NSH'), is exploring future procurement activities related to Short Term Illness (STI) Case Management.
The purpose of this NPP is to provide interested suppliers with an opportunity to participate in a one‑to‑one Commercially Confidential Meeting ('CCM') with the Province and NSH. These meetings are intended to support early market engagement and to allow suppliers to share insights, service models, and innovations in advance of any formal procurement.
