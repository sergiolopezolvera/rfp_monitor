Description:
MODY is issuing this Request for Proposals (RFP) to identify qualified consulting firms or individuals to, for a three-year period, support the CAO Committee and Council with facilitation services for the annual performance management and goal setting of the Chief Administrative Officer.
The successful Proponent is responsible for working closely with the Chief Administrative Officer and the CAO Committee to develop an annual performance evaluation process. The final process and associated tools will be approved by MODY Council. The Proponent will then annually facilitate the process.
