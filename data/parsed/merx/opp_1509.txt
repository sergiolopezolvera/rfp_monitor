Description:
The City of Grande Prairie (the “City”) is requesting detailed submissions from qualified and experienced proponents (the “Consultant”) to develop a Reclamation Plan for the Grande Prairie Airport Lagoon system (“Airport Lagoon”). The Reclamation Plan is required under Alberta’s Code of Practice for Wastewater Systems Using a Wastewater Lagoon and must be submitted to Alberta Environment and Protected Areas (AEPA) within the required regulatory timelines.
