This Request for Proposals (the “RFP”) is an invitation by Build Nova Scotia (“BNS”) to prospective proponents to submit proposals for BNS-2627-0025 RFP for Former Lake Charlotte Mine Site Phase I/II ESA
BNS is seeking the services of a consultant with experienced and qualified site professionals, senior environmental experts, and field personnel to conduct environmental investigations of the former Lake Charlotte gold mine, in order to provide Phase I/II ESA reports and a Class D remediation estimates for the Crown Land Properties.
