ANBL & CNB are inviting prospective proponents to submit proposals for end-to-end Microsoft Dynamics 365 IT support services to include enhancements and support during regular hours and after-hours for the organizations.
Documents are posted on/proposals must be submitted through:
https://anbl.bonfirehub.ca/opportunities
and shall be received up to 1:30 pm on May 22nd ,2026.
There is a question period that ends on May 5th,2026 at 4:30 pm and there will be an addendum issued by May 7th,2026 with a list of all questions received and the responses provided. All questions must be submitted through the Opportunity Q&A within Bonfire, prior to the Deadline for Questions. No such communications are to be sent or initiated through any other means.

and Regulation 2014-93 as of the date of the issuance of the Invitation.
