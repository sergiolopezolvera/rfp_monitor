Description:
The Regional District of Okanagan-Similkameen (RDOS) is seeking proposals from qualified professionals (QP) to complete a feasibility study and cost-based assessment of the benefits of combining the Skaha Estates and Lakeshore Water systems with options to include connections to the Okanagan Falls Water system and Kaleden Irrigation District. The combining of systems will include the design and construction of a new water treatment plant, and deep-water intake. All systems are within proximity at the southern extent of Skaha lake. The successful Respondent will be required to analyze the existing systems in their current condition, analyze data to assess required upgrades, and determine required maintenance.
Respondents are encouraged to use innovation to provide a proposal that meets the needs of the Regional District and adds additional value to the project.
