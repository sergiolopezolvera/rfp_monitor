AI Overview:
Professional expertise is sought for land use planning, specifically for reviewing and updating municipal planning strategies and associated land use bylaws, including review of existing bylaws, strategies, policies, reports, and relevant provincial and federal legislation.

Description:
The project objective is to obtain professional expertise in the area of land use planning and more specifically in the development of municipal planning strategies and associated land use bylaws to complete a review and update of the current MPS/LUB. The successful candidate will be expected to review all existing bylaws, strategies, policies and reports as well as existing provincial and federal legislation that may have an impact on land use planning matters.
