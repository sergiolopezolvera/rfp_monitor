The Local Government is seeking a qualified Supplier to provide routine police vehicle cleaning, biological decontamination, and biohazard remediation services for the police fleet. Police vehicles are high#contact environments that may be exposed to bodily fluids, biological contaminants, and other hazardous materials through daily operational use.
The intent of this Scope of Services is to ensure police vehicles are maintained in a clean, sanitary, and operationally safe condition, while ensuring that any biological or biohazard contamination is addressed by trained and properly equipped personnel using industry#recognized practices.
