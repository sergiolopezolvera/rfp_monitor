Description:
The District is seeking proposals from experienced and qualified moving service providers to support a wide range of relocation, delivery, and internal move requirements across its facilities. Reliable and professional moving services are a vital component of our day-to-day operations, directly supporting schools, departments, and programs by ensuring that furniture, equipment, and resources are moved safely, efficiently, and with minimal disruption. The successful proponent will demonstrate the capacity, expertise, and flexibility required to meet ongoing operational needs in a dynamic, service-driven environment.
