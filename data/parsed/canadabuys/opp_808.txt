Description:
The City of Grande Prairie (the “City”) is requesting detailed submissions from qualified and experienced Proponents (the “Proponent”) to provide the design, fabrication, delivery, and installation of the exterior building signage for the Grande Prairie Police Service (“GPPS”) Headquarter building. Requiring up to four (4) signs, subject to cost considerations. Requiring two (2) GPPS‑branded ground signs, and up to two (2) illuminated building‑mounted police identification signage (“POLICE” lettering), suitable for exterior use and consistent with common police identification signage practices.
