Description:
Canadian Forces Base (CFB) Kingston – Logistic Support Squadron (LSS) Supply Platoon - Clothing Stores - Royal Military College (RMC) Clothing Stores Detachment has a requirement for tailoring and clothing alteration services. This service is required to support and augment the RMC tailors during the First Year RMC Cadet intake where more than two hundred and fifty to three hundred (250 - 300) new RMC Cadets will be measured and issued their uniforms between August 25nd to September 2nd, 2026 in support of Reunion Weekend which occurs annually. The requirement is for three firm years with two optional one year periods.
