Description of the Requirement:
The Future Aircrew Training (FAcT) Program has issued a Contract to SkyAlyne for the operation of pilot training programs in Moose Jaw, Southport, and Winnipeg. DND has a requirement for a Task Based Informatics Professional Services (TBIPS) Contractor to provide the services of a Senior Business Process Re-Engineering Consultant, a Senior Business Consultant, a Senior Strategic Information Technology Security Planning and Protection Consultant and an Intermediate Information Technology Security TRA and C&A Analyst.
SkyAlyne has started the implementation of the FAcT training system. The TBIPS Contractor will support the training system implementation by acting as a liaison between the end-client (DND), the FAcT team, key stakeholders and SkyAlyne. As part of that contract, SkyAlyne is the System Implementor (SI) and during the Pre-Service Delivery phase, has started the Implementation of the FAcT Training System. To support the Implementation work, a TBIPS contract is required to provide the DND with contracted IM/IT Subject Matter Expert (SME) professional services resources to act as liaison between the end-client, the FAcT Team, Key Stakeholders, and the SI.
