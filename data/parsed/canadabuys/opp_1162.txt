Description:
The Town of Canmore is seeking a multidisciplinary consulting team to develop We Are Winter: A Winter Town Strategy, a comprehensive plan to enhance the winter experience, strengthen the winter economy, and improve accessibility and experience for residents, businesses, and visitors. The strategy will provide coordinated, community-informed guidance to help Canmore better plan, design, and activate winter environments while aligning municipal policies and operations with winter realities.
The successful proponent will work closely with Town staff and community partners to deliver an actionable, forward-looking framework that supports year-round vibrancy and long-term operational and economic resilience.
