Description:
The Calgary Board of Education (CBE) invites your firm to submit your bid on this Request for Proposal (RFP) for the supply of complete maintenance for the elevators and lifts operated by the CBE at various schools.
The service shall include all labour, materials and equipment necessary for the making of regular examinations of our elevators and lifts in accordance with the CSA-B44 and CSA-B55 elevator codes, including annual CAT1 testing, CAT5 testing (traction elevators), oiling, cleaning, and greasing, and other preventive maintenance tasks.
