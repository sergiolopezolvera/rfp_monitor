Description:
The Province is requesting the submission of Proposals for Outhouse/Caretaking services for the following outhouses along the David Thomson Corridor (DTC), Cow lake and Strubel Lake:
• Nordegg Ranger Station Parking Lot
• Colliseum Staging Area
• Bighorn Dam
• Allstones Staging Area
• Allstones Random Camping Area
• Hoodoo Creek East Staging Area
• Hoodoo Creek West Staging Area
• Ahbraham Slabs Staging Area
• Whitegoat/Coral Creek Staging Area
• Pinto Lake Staging Area
• Preachers Point Random Camping Areas
• Kinglette Lake
• Wildhorse East
• Wildhorse West
• Owen Creek
• Thompson Creek Staging Area
• Taunton Parking Area
• Chambers Creek Staging Area
• Landslide Lake Interpretive Trail
• Cline Landing
• Cow Lake
• Strubel Lake
The Outhouse/Caretaking services for facilities along the David Thomson Corridor (DTC), Cow Lake and Strubel Lake consists of the furnishing of all the labour, supplies, and equipment required for the cleaning of the Outhouses, emptying of Garbage and Recycle Bins as shown and with the frequency noted in Appendix B, Table 1. Pricing for cleaning supplies, consumable washroom products, and plastic garbage bags shall be supplied to the Province on a cost plus basis, with the Province reserving the right to purchase these materials directly outside of this contract. Cleaning products and washroom supplies shall be deemed environmentally friendly, and approved by the Province. All other supplies necessary to complete the tasks will be provided by the Contractor.
