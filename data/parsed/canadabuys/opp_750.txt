Description:
The purpose of these standing offers (SOs) is to enable a large number of federal public servants (learners) with various language profiles/levels across the country to begin, continue or finish learning English as a second language (ESL) or French as a second language (FSL) for oral proficiency, as well as to maintain their skills in this language.
