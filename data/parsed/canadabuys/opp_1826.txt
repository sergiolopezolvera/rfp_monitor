Description:
The intent of this Request for Proposal (RFP) is to increase employment opportunities for persons with developmental disabilities by facilitating access to skills training and employment supports. This RFP builds an integrated, client-centered, outcomes–focused training and employment model. Services will be tailored to each person to minimize barriers.
There are the Employment Services and the Youth Employment Services for the North and South Zone. There will be up to four contracts awarded for these services.
