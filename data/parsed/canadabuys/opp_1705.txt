Description:
The Department of National Defence (DND), 19 Wing Comox, has a requirement for Hazardous Waste Disposal Services. The work will involve the supply of labor, material equipment, tools and supervision required for the loading, transportation and disposal/destruction/treatment of all hazardous waste products listed in Annex “Basis of Payment” of the RFP.
