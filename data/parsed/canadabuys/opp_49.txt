Description:
The purpose of this Statement of Work is to provide the Canadian Forces Health Services Group (CF H Svcs Gp), and the Canadian Forces Drug Exception Centre (CF DEC) with Drug Information Services (DIS). The DIS will be provided to DND pharmacists across 33 sites, in order to enhance their access to specialized drug information resources. These services must be provided to Canadian Forces pharmacists in both English and French, and in a timely and accurate manner all year-round.
