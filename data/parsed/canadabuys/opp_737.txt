Description:
Department of National Defence (DND) has a requirement for a Repair and Overhaul contract to be established to support its AN/PVS-14 Night Vision capability platform. AN/PVS-14 NVGs will be retrofitted with state of the art Image Intensifier Tubes which will enhance the night vision capabilities of CAF. The contract will be for an initial period of two years from Contract award date with an additional three (3) years of 1 year option periods.
