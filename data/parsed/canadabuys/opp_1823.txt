Description:
ARROW is seeking a reliable and experienced service provider capable of performing preventive maintenance, inspection, troubleshooting, repair, emergency response, and related support services for compressed air systems and associated equipment located at ARROW Utilities facilities and pump stations.
The scope of services includes scheduled preventive maintenance for compressors and dryers, on-call emergency repair services, condition assessments, reporting, and coordination of temporary backup compressor services where required during equipment outages or replacement activities.
