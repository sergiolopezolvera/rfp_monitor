Description:
The objective of this Request for Proposal (RFP) is to identify and engage a qualified vendor to operate and maintain The City’s audio-visual and related technologies used for Council meetings, Committee meetings, administrative tribunal hearings, and other special or ad-hoc events. The selected vendor must ensure that all such meetings, hearings, and events are effectively supported with appropriate technologies to facilitate, broadcast, and record the proceedings.
