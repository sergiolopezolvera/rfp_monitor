Description:
This Request for Quotations (the “RFQ”) is an invitation by the City of St. Albert (the “City”) to prospective respondents to submit quotations for Geotechnical Investigation Community Amenities Site, as further described in Section A of the RFQ Particulars (Appendix B) (the “Deliverables”).
The City is looking to perform six deep (20-30m) geotechnical test holes to confirm all geotechnical information and characteristics required for the design and construction of the new community amenities facility.
