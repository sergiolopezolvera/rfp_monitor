Description:
In its search for the most economical means of accessing research on its priority issues, the Government of Canada (GC) is looking for syndicated public opinion research studies that could address its research needs. On behalf of the GC, the Public Opinion Research Directorate is seeking detailed information through a Request for Information (RFI) on the studies that are available for purchase with a government-wide subscription. Please note that only studies offering a government-wide subscription will be considered.
