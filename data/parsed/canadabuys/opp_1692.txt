Description:
The Special Areas Board (SAB) is requesting proposals from qualified proponents to provide professional media content collection services to support updates to SAB’s corporate brand, marketing materials, website, social media, and broader communications efforts.
The intent of this project is to refresh and strengthen the Special Areas Board brand story, reflecting the region’s core values of being Rural Remote, Resourceful, and Resilient, and to ensure consistent, high quality visual content across digital and marketing channels.
