Description:
The City of Lloydminster is looking to engage a qualified Consultant to develop a design for the redevelopment of an existing tot pool located at the Co-op Community Outdoor Pool into an interactive spray park. The goal is to create a safe, accessible, and engaging aquatic play space that better meets the needs of young children and families while improving functionality and long-term sustainability of the facility.
