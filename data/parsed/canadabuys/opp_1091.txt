Description:
Canada School of Public Service (CSPS) has a service requirement for Sign language (American Sign Language (ASL) and Langue des signes Québécoise (LSQ)) Services for upcoming events organized by CSPS, on an ‘’as and when requested’’ basis. Up to two (2) contracts could be awarded. If contracts are awarded, the Period of the contracts will be for one (1) year from contract award. Two (2) irrevocable options to extend the term of the contract by one (1) year each form an integral part of the contract.
The total allocated budget for this requirement (including 2 option years) is $330,000.00, excluding applicable taxes. $110,000.00 for the initial contract (one year from contract award date) and $110,000.00 per option year.
There is no security requirement applicable to the contract.
