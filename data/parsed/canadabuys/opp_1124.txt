Description:
The objectives of this RFP are to:
- Ensure that the Province has access to a qualified DNA Testing Service for CSS program Clients.
- Ensure the Province has access to a DNA Testing Service that has the appropriate accreditation, qualifications, and expertise that will allow the testing results to withstand any potential court challenge.
- Establish DNA Testing Service fees that will be maintained during the term of the Contract and any possible extensions.
