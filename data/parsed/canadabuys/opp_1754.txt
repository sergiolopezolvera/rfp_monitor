Description:
The City of Grande Prairie (the “City”) invites proposals from engineering firms specializing in urban road rehabilitation for the 2027–2028 Road Overlay and 2026–2028 Paved Lane Rehabilitation programs. The successful proponent (the “Consultant”) will manage the full project lifecycle—including geotechnical assessment, detailed design, tender preparation, construction supervision, and warranty services—ensuring all work meets or exceeds the City Design and Construction Manuals.
