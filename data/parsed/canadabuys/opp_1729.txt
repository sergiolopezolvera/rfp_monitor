Description:
The Calgary Board of Education (CBE) requires the services of a qualified vendor(s) to provide swimming pool maintenance services at two (2) school sites. All services must be performed in compliance with the Alberta Health Pool Standards (January 2018).
The successful Contractor shall supply all required equipment, materials, chemicals, labour, and supervision necessary to deliver the services outlined in Schedule “A” – Scope of Work contained in the RFP package. The scope of work includes, but is not limited to, the following:
-Daily service requirements on weekdays during the school year
-Weekly service requirements
-Draining each pool at the end of June and refilling each pool at the end of August
-Maintenance of required log books
