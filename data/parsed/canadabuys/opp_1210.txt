Description:
The City of Calgary is seeking a qualified firm to perform inspection, certification, maintenance and repair services for cranes, hoists and other lifting devices, as well as regulated fall protection equipment. Scope of work may also include equipment replacement when required.
Supplier will be responsible to ensure all cranes, hoists, lifting equipment, and regulated fall protection equipment are safe, compliant with Provincial legislation and fully operational through qualified inspections, maintenance, repair, and replacement services.
