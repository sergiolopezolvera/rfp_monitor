Description:
The City of Calgary is seeking professional consulting services to develop Asset Management Plans for its existing Water Treatment Plants and to establish Design for Reliability Guidelines to support future water treatment facility planning and design. The work will inform risk‑based lifecycle management, long‑term investment decisions, and the delivery of safe, reliable drinking water services.
