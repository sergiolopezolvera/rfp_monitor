Description:
Invest Greater Calgary (IGC) is seeking to develop a professional digital platform that positions the Calgary Capital Region as one of North America’s leading locations for business expansion, investment, and market entry.
The purpose of this project is to design, develop, and launch a high-quality investment attraction website that serves as IGC’s primary marketing, communications, and business development platform. The website will be designed to engage international and North American business leaders, corporate executives, investors, site selectors, and expansion teams who are evaluating opportunities in Canada and Western Canada.
This project is intended to communicate the region’s competitive advantages, showcase priority industry opportunities, and clearly demonstrate how IGC supports companies throughout the expansion and investment process.
Ultimately, this initiative is intended to establish a credible, investor-focused digital presence that supports long-term foreign direct investment (FDI), business attraction, and regional economic growth across the Calgary Capital Region.
