Project Description: On behalf of Correctional Services Canada (CSC), Public Works and Government Services Canada (PWGSC) is requesting the services of an Architectural Consultant to redesign the kitchen area at the Atlantic Institution in Renous, New Brunswick. This is a request for proposals (RFP) against Architecture Supply Arrangement (SA) E0225-152290. The entire list of firms qualified on the A4 discipline for the province of Nova Scotia have been invited to submit a proposal. Debrief: Bidders may request a debriefing on the results of the bid solicitation. Bidders should make the request to the Contracting Authority within fifteen (15) working days from receipt of notification that their bid was unsuccessful. General Information: Proposals must be submitted only via SAP Ariba by the date and time indicated in the RFP. The RFP issuing office is: Atlantic Region Acquisitions, 1713 Bedford Row, Halifax, N.S., B3J 1T3. This office provides services in English. Proposals may be submitted in either official language of Canada.
