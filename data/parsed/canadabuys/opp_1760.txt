Description:
The Town of Coalhurst is issuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) to solicit submissions from qualified individuals, companies or municipalities to provide Solid Waste Collection and Disposal Services. The initial contract term will run from October 1, 2026, to October 1, 2029, with the option to extend for up to two additional one-year periods at the Town’s discretion.
The Town’s Residential Collection Program currently services approximately 1,100 residential dwelling units. The successful proponent will be responsible for delivering dependable, efficient, and high-quality collection services while maintaining a strong focus on customer service.
