Description:
Canada is seeking bids to provide catering services for the Department of National Defence (DND).
The contractor will be required to provide catering services for an estimated thirty-seven (37) members. The required services include the provision of three (3) meals per day, from Monday to Friday.
The contractor is responsible for supplying all food and non-food items, as well as delivering the meals three (3) times per day.
The contract period is from May 25 to August 21, 2026, inclusive.
