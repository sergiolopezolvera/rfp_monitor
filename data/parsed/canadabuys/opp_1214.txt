Description:
The Health Protection Branch of the Public Health Delivery Division of Primary and Preventative Health Services (PPHS) is responsible for overseeing environmental public health in Alberta through defining policy and providing direction to the programs of the Government of Alberta (GoA) ministries and agencies.
The purpose of this engagement is to provide support to fully develop and implement the data management frameworks, to tackle the current backlog of datasets waiting to be addressed, maintain data from routine monitoring programs, manage datasets that EPHS receives from other agencies and use internally, and maintain surge capacity during responses to unplanned situations and incidents.
Objectives includes:
1. Dataset Quality Optimization
2. Data Governance and Stewardship
3. Dataset Development and Maintenance
4. Public Access and Transparency
5. EPHS Deliverables and Products
6. Program Support and Continuous Improvement
