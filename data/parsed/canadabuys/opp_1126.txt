Description:
The County of Stettler No. 6, in partnership with the County of Paintearth No. 18, Starland County, the Special Areas Board, and the Province of Alberta, is undertaking a feasibility study and conceptual design to determine a practical and cost-effective path forward for Boehlke’s Pond.
Boehlke’s Pond is a provincially owned site that has experienced infrastructure deterioration and has been inactive since 2017.
The study will evaluate two primary options:
• Rehabilitation as a recreational fishery and park
• Conversion to a naturalized wetland
As further detailed in the Project Scope, the project will result in:
• A recommended preferred option
• Conceptual design
• Class D cost estimates
• An implementation and regulatory roadmap
The study will support decision making, future funding applications, and long-term management of the site.
The intent of this study is to support progression to capital implementation, subject to a viable preferred option.
