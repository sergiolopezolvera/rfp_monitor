Description:
The Town of Drumheller (the “Town”) seeks innovative proposals from interested parties for the following:
The Town invites proposals to remove accumulated sludge from Cell 1, located at the Town’s wastewater treatment facility. Dewatering of the recovered sludge and disposing of solids at an approved facility will be required. The desludging process is expected to occur while the lagoon remains in service while also protecting the in‑situ aeration system, piping and liner. The 2025 sludge survey identified approximately 2,737 m³ of sludge (about 76% of the cell’s volume).
Proponents shall propose a compliant disposal pathway (e.g., Class II landfill or other) and describe how filtrate generated during dewatering will be safely managed.
