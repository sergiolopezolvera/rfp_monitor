Description:
This RFP seeks to retain the services of one or more companies specializing in commercial real estate brokerage to assist the Corporation in leasing certain available rental spaces on the Old Port of Montréal site. Services may include marketing, commercial lease negotiation, and any other complementary services required for effective brokerage that maximize the value of the assets to be brought to market.
The mandate aims to:
• maximize the rental value and performance of leasable spaces,
• engage potential tenants and partners using a professional and proactive strategy,
• advise the Corporation on strategic leasing and space management decisions, and
• provide end-to-end management of the process, including marketing, coordination, and recommendations.
