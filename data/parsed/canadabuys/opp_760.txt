Description:
Public Service and Procurement Canada on behalf of the Department of National Defence– (Halifax) has a requirement for a Service Contract that comprises the furnishing of all labour, materials, tools, equipment, transportation and supervision required to perform the inspection, cleaning, calibration and maintenance of Petroleum, Oil and Lubrication (POL) dispensing units, pumps, flow meters, level as specified in the Statement of Work. The requested period for this service contract is for one year from August 1,2026 to July 31,2027 with an additional two (2) one (1) year period options available. This solicitation of offers is open only to Canadian suppliers and suppliers of an Applicable Trading Partner. Documents may be submitted in either official language of Canada.
