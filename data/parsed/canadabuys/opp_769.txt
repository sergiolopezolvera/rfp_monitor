The Department of Fisheries and Oceans Canada (DFO) has a requirement for moving services which includes labour, material, equipment, tools, supervision and transportation required for the move of 22 staff in Iqaluit, Nunavut. This contract may require recycling of furniture/items and the move of surplus items to surplus staging areas and locations.
