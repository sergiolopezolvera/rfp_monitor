Nature of Requirements:
The following is a summary of the statement of work for this requirement.
The Correctional Service Canada (CSC), Health Services requires Dental services for the following Institutions in the Quebec Region: Donnacona, Cowansville and LaMacaza Institutions, and the Federal Training Centre (site 600 and 6099).
Objectives: The Contractor must provide primary dental care, including x-rays and health promotion, to inmates that complies with relevant federal legislation, provincial standards and CSC policies and guidelines.
Deliverables: To provide primary dental care during the clinic hours indicated in Annex B – Proposed Basis of Payment over a three (3) year period.
