Description:
Canada is seeking offers from Offerors to provide Water Transport Training on an as and when requested basis using Task Authorizations to ensure its members receive proper training in marine vessels operation and execution of marine policing duties in accordance with the RCMP Course Training Standard (CTS) as well as the competencies required by Transport Canada (TC) for operators of marine vessels. The Contract will be for a period of 2 years with 2 one year options.
