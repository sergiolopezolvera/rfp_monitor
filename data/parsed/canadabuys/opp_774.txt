Nature of Requirements:
The following is a summary of the statement of work for this requirement.
The Correctional Service of Canada requires clinical dental care for inmates at the Saskatchewan Penitentiary
Objectives: to provide professional dental services to inmates at the Saskatchewan Penitentiary in order to support offender health care needs in accordance with applicable legislation, professional standards, and Correctional Service Canada policies.
Deliverables: The Contractor must provide on site clinical dental services, including assessment, diagnosis, treatment, and related dental care activities, as described in the Statement of Work. The Contractor must provide services in accordance with professional licensing requirements and institutional operational requirements.
