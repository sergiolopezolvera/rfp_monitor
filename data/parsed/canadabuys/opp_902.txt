Description:
The Municipal District of Opportunity (MD) requests written proposals from qualified vendors to provide services for a new SCADA system. This is for the hamlets of Wabasca, Calling Lake, Red Earth Creek, and Sandy Lake. This will be a new VT SCADA system connecting all the lift stations of each community to their respective Water Treatment Plant. Requiring a completely independent network, with each lift station able to communicate independently with their respective Water Treatment Plants.
