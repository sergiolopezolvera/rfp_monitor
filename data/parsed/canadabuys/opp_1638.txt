Description:
The Crossroads Economic Development Alliance (CEDA), a regional economic development partnership. The Alliance is comprised of the County of Minburn, Town of Vegreville, Village of Innisfree, and Village of Mannville, and is supported by the Northeast Alberta Information HUB.
The Crossroads Regional Investment Readiness Project will develop enhanced communication strategies and tools across the County of Minburn region, supporting communities, organizations, and businesses in strengthening investment readiness and attraction efforts. This collaborative approach will help the four partner municipalities and their business communities better target new investment opportunities, while positioning the region as an attractive place to work, live, and visit.
