Description:
This pilot project by Alberta Assisted Living and Social Services (ALSS) is intended to obtain the procurement of an appointment-based virtual career coaching service for 36 months (plus possible extensions), that leverages and supports the career development tools and resources available on the Province-wide alis.alberta.ca (alis) website.
