Description:
Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) is the custodian of the Shirleys Bay Campus, which includes laboratories supporting research and development in advanced telecommunications systems. The campus consists of approximately 62 permanent buildings and 60 temporary buildings.
The Communications Research Centre (CRC), a branch of ISED, requires Waste Management Services in accordance with all applicable federal, provincial, and municipal regulations.
The Contractor must provide all labour, equipment, containers, and supervision necessary to perform waste, recycling, and organic collection services for twelve (12) designated pick-up sites at the Shirleys Bay Campus, located at 3701 Carling Avenue, Ottawa, ON.
