Description:
The Town of Fort Macleod invites qualified consulting firms to submit proposals for the delivery of professional services to develop a comprehensive Airport Master Plan for Fort Macleod Airport (CEY3), located at 367 Avro Anson Road, Fort Macleod, Alberta.
The Airport is owned and operated by the Town and currently does not have an existing Master Plan. The Town is seeking an actionable and realistic overarching document to guide the airport’s development and growth.
