Description:
The Department of National Defence (DND) has a requirement for one (1) resource who will provide the expertise of the Learning Advisor at the senior level to provide professional support to the Future Fighter Lead-In Training (FFLIT) Training Development Team.
The Learning Advisor will supplement and enhance the functional capability of the Training Development Team by providing expert advice, analysis, and support related to military aviation training systems, curriculum development, instructional design, training validation, and training effectiveness evaluation.
