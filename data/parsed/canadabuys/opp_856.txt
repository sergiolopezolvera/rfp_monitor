Description:
The Province is requesting the submission of Proposals for Outhouse vault cleaning Services for the following outhouses along the David Thomson Corridor (DTC), Cow lake and Strubel Lake:
• Nordegg Ranger Station Parking Lot
• Colliseum Staging Area
• Bighorn Dam
• Allstones Staging Area
• Allstones Random Camping Area
• Hoodoo Creek East Staging Area
• Hoodoo Creek West Staging Area
• Ahbraham Slabs Staging Area
• Whitegoat/Coral Creek Staging Area
• Pinto Lake Staging Area
• Preachers Point Random Camping Areas
• Kinglette Lake
• Wildhorse East
• Wildhorse West
• Owen Creek
• Thompson Creek Staging Area
• Taunton Parking Area
• Chambers Creek Staging Area
• Landslide Lake Interpretive Trail
• Cline Landing
• Cow Lake
• Strubel Lake
The Outhouse vault services for facilities along the David Thomson Corridor (DTC), Cow Lake and Strubel Lake consists of the furnishing of all the labour, supplies, and equipment required for the cleaning of the Outhouse vaults with the cleaning generally done, but not limited to, the fall (October/November). However, the frequency noted in Appendix B, Table 1 is stated as variable, as there may be additional times requested to clean that is to be started/completed within 48 hrs of notice from the Contract Manager. Cleaning products and washroom supplies shall be deemed environmentally friendly, and approved by The Province. All other supplies necessary to complete the tasks will be provided by the Contractor.
