Description:
Amendment 1 - Update trade agreements and add additional UNSPSC codes.
The Department of National Defence has a requirement for the lease of quantity 600 UHF handheld radios with batteries. The product must include 1x Programming Cable for every 6x radios, 1x Headset, 1x Carrying case with belt clip or equivalent, 1x Spare Battery and 1x Multi Bay charger with minimum 6x charging slots for every 6x radios. UHF handheld radio must have a minimum frequency range of 403 Mhz – 470 Mhz.
