Description:
Please note that late submissions will not be considered or accepted. The Department of National Defence (DND), Canadian Forces Base (CFB) Cold Lake, 4 Wing, requires the supply and delivery of liquid Aviator’s Breathing Oxygen (ABO) and liquid nitrogen (LN₂), as required. This requirement includes the provision, installation, operation, and management of contractor owned on site storage tanks for liquid oxygen (LOX) and liquid nitrogen (LN₂), including all associated systems. This solicitation of offers is open only to Canadian suppliers and to Suppliers of an Applicable Trading Partner. An optional site visit will be held on April 22, 2026, beginning at 1:30PM MT. Canada recommends that the Offeror attend the site visit. To attend the site visit, Offerors must communicate with the Standing Offer Authority no later than April 6, 2026 at 4:00PM MT to confirm attendance and provide the required information of their representatives in order to gain entry to CFB 4Wing. Please see 5.2. Site Visit. on page 9 of the RFSO for full registration information. Offerors who do not attend will not be given an alternative appointment but they will not be precluded from submitting an offer.
