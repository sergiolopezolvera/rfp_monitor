Description:
The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) intends to enter into one contract with one contractor for the Health Assessment Services (HAS)
This requirement also includes options to extend the contract up to six (6) additional one-year periods under the same conditions.
The period of the Contract is from the contract award date to August 31, 2028 inclusive.
A bid must comply with the requirements of the bid solicitation and meet all mandatory evaluation criteria to be declared responsive. The responsive bid with the highest combined rating of technical merit and price will be recommended for award of a contract.
Proposal documents and supporting information must be submitted in either English or French.
