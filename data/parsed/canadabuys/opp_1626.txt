Description:
Public Services and Procurement Canada, Atlantic Region, has a requirement to provide laundry, kitchen and galley equipment/appliance maintenance, including inspection, repair, replacement, and maintenance services, both scheduled and an “as and when requested” basis. The Contractor must bring the equipment up to operational capabilities, in accordance with the manufacturers’ specifications.
The standing offer term will be two years. The Atlantic region has been broken down into geographic delivery zones. One standing offer will be awarded for each zone.
This solicitation of offers is open only to Canadian suppliers and to Suppliers of an applicable trading partner.
Offerors may request a debriefing on the results of the solicitation of offers process.
Reference the solicitation for further details.
