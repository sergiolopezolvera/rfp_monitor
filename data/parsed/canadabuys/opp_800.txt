Description:
The Public Library Services Branch (PLSB) of Alberta Municipal Affairs seeks to acquire a cloud-based solution that will enable interlibrary loan between public libraries in Alberta.
Please refer to the Request for Proposal (RFP) document, "RFP PLSB-2026-001", for detailed information about:
• the mandatory requirements and desirable functionality of the solution;
• how to prepare, format, and submit a proposal; and,
• the criteria and process for evaluating proposals.
