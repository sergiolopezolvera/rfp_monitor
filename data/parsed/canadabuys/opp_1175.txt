Description:
The Government of Canada (GoC) requires the rental, delivery, positioning and removal of infrastructure equipment (such as office trailers, shipping containers, portable toilets, generators, lighting trailers, power panels, and fencing) and the related support services to establish temporary sites in three (3) parking lots at the University of British Columbia for the period between May 11, 2026 and July 26, 2026. The rental period will vary depending on the equipment.
There is an optional site visit scheduled for May 1, 2026 at 10:00PDT. See Amendment 001.
