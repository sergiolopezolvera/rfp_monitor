Description:
The Municipality of Jasper is seeking proposals for Recovery Marketing Services under the Jasper Commerce Continuity Initiative (CCI). This project focuses on delivering targeted, on-the-ground marketing initiatives that drive customer activity and support local businesses impacted by the 2024 wildfire.
The successful proponent will develop and implement practical, conversion-focused tactics aimed at visitors already in Jasper, those with near-term travel plans, and local/regional audiences. The work emphasizes increasing visibility, foot traffic, and spending at businesses operating within the Pop-Up Village and surrounding commercial areas.
The total all-inclusive budget for this project is $50,000 CAD over a 20-month term.
