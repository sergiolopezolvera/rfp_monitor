Description:
For the Canadian Coast Guard Bay Class Search and Rescue vessels, the scope of work includes design, integration, testing, Class certification and supply of kits required to add an Emergency Declutch System that is fully compatible with, and controlled through, the current Kobelt propulsion control architecture. In accordance with the Shipbuilding and Repair Policy, sourcing is limited to suppliers who are Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) Kobelt‑Authorized Distributors and Service Centres for these SAR vessels in Canada.
