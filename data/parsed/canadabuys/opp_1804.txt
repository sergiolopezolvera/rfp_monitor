Description:
Action 5 of the Climate Resilience Planning and Development (CRPD) Action Plan is to update the Floodplain Protection Overlay (FPO) in the Edmonton Zoning Bylaw.
The consultant will advise on incorporating climate projections into Edmonton's Floodplain Protection Overlay map using Alberta's Flood Hazard Risk maps and establishing boundaries for a “high hazard flood area” and a “medium hazard flood area.”
