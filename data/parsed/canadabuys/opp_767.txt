Description of the Requirement:
The Department of Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs Canada (CIRNAC) has a requirement for the independent review of Article 23 of the Nunavut Agreement. The review will consist of a detailed analysis and assessment of Inuit Employment Plans and other measures taken under Article 23 covering the period of 2016 to 2023. The Contractor is expected to produce a written report, which should include a detailed analysis and assessment of actions taken by Government and Inuit to implement Article 23 between 2016 and 2023. This report will be used to guide the Nunavut Implementation Panel to develop recommendations to the parties about how best to improve implementation efforts going forward.
