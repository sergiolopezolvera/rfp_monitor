Requirement Description
Canada is seeking offers from Offerors for the aacquisition of Credits From Carbon Dioxide Removal Projects to Reduce Net-Greenhouse Gas Emissions across multiple technology work streams from CDR projects that meet the government’s technical requirements:
Stream 1: Direct Air Carbon Dioxide Capture and Permanent Storage (DACCS).
Stream 2: Bioenergy with Carbon Capture and Storage (BECCS).
Stream 3: Biochar.
Stream 4: Other Biomass Carbon Removal and Storage (BiCRS).
Stream 5: Enhanced Mineralization.
Each technology stream will be the subject of a separate competitive process.
The CDR project and any associated CO2 storage activities must be located in Canada.
Expected Contract Period:
The period of the SO will be between award day and March 31, 2029.
