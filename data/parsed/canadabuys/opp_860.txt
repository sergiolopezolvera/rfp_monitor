The Calgary Board of Education (CBE) is seeking a cloud-native, AI-driven cybersecurity solution to defend its 150,000+ students and 17,000+ staff against advanced threats like phishing and account takeovers. Moving beyond traditional signature-based tools, the CBE requires a self-learning system capable of behavioural, language-based, and contextual analysis.
