Nature of Requirements:
The following is a summary of the statement of work for this requirement.
The Correctional Service Canada has a requirement to provide Dental services for Joyceville Institution in the Ontario Region.
Objectives: The Contractor must provide Dental services to inmates and collaborate with the institution’s multi-disciplinary health services team that includes but is not limited to, Nurses, Institutional Physicians, Psychologists, Dieticians and other allied health professionals.
Deliverables: The Contractor must provide and coordinate essential dental services to inmates at Joyceville Institution.
