Description:
The County of Stettler No. 6 is looking to update its orthophoto coverage of the entire County.
The proposal shall consist of information for creating orthophotos in two options:
• 10 cm resolution
• 20 cm resolution
The proposal shall also provide pricing for the option of creating 0.5m contours.
The intent of this project is to update the current orthophotos to more accurately reflect what is on the ground and changes to development within the County.
