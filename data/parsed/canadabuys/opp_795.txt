Description:
A Self‑Employment program can be delivered in person, online through live sessions, or through a mix of both.
The program can be up to 26 weeks in total and must cover all major parts of running a business, including:
• Instructor‑Led Training: Participants receive training and learning materials on how to start and operate a business, including developing a business plan.
• Business Implementation: Participants receive support and mentoring as they begin operating their business. This includes one‑to‑one counselling, coaching, guidance on capital needs, and support during business start‑up. On‑site monitoring is included to help ensure the business plan is put into action successfully.
• The combined length of Instructor‑Led Training and Business Implementation must not exceed 26 weeks.
• By the end of the program, participants are expected to be operating their business full‑time and be self‑sufficient.
• Participants will continue to receive support, guidance, and service management throughout the program and for 90 days after completing it.
