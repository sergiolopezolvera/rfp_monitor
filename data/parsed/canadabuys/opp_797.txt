Description:
The City of Wetaskiwin (the “City”) is seeking a qualified consulting partner to deliver
Phase 0 services for the replacement of the City’s Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
system. The consultant will conduct ERP visioning and strategic planning, lead
requirements development, prepare a comprehensive ERP System Replacement RFP,
provide evaluation tools and demonstration scripts, and facilitate the subsequent
procurement and negotiations with ERP vendors. The scope also includes an optional,
separately priced add-on for implementation advisory services during the ERP
implementation phase.
