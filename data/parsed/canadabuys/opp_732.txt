Description of the Requirement:
The Department of National Defence (DND) has a requirement for the services of one (1) System Administrator Level 3 and one (1) System Administrator Level 2.) to support all aspects of the operation of the Mapping and Charting Establishment (MCE) network capabilities. These include Data Centre operations, server virtualization, server administration, storage systems administration including installation, configuration, optimization, maintenance, testing and troubleshooting, network configuration and administration.
