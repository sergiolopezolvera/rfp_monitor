Description:
The Town is seeking proposals for the supply, implementation, and ongoing support of a modern telecommunications system to replace the existing phone system. The proposed solution must provide a reliable, flexible, and user-friendly platform that supports current operational requirements and can adapt to future organizational needs.
The phone system is a core operational tool used across multiple departments and facilities, supporting both internal communication and public access. As such, the solution must be dependable, easy to use, and capable of supporting a range of user types and service environments. The system must maintain consistent performance and support a high standard of service delivery to both staff and the public.
The Town is open to a range of solution types, including cloud-hosted, on-premises, or hybrid systems. Solutions may include physical desk phones, software-based applications, mobile devices, or a combination of these. Proponents are encouraged to propose solutions that best meet the Town’s needs while demonstrating long-term sustainability, scalability, and overall value.
