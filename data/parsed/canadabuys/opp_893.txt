Description:
Edmonton Public Schools owns and operates over 230 facilities serving approximately 125,000 students.
The Division Integrated Infrastructure Services (IIS), is responsible for these sites and along with continual operational maintenance, provides renewal (IMR) and capital (CMR) construction project support. In addition, IIS handles school generated program-based renovations.
This competitive RFP seeks to solicit qualification proposals from qualified Audio-Visual Consultants with Electrical Consulting Engineers interested in working on the Division’s gymnasium and other large-scale audio-visual projects.
