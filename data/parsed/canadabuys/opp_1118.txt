Description:
The Town of Stony Plain is seeking proposals from a qualified and experienced contractor for the supply and replacement or upgrade of residential, commercial and industrial water meters. The scope of work includes:
• Replacement of existing meters near the end of their useful life.
• Design, supply, installation and calibration of AMR infrastructure (RF Transmitters, Meter Reading Devices, Meter Route processing software etc.)
• Retrofit existing meters that require Radio Transmitters for implementation of AMR system.
• Provide turnkey solution to the Town including project management, implementation plan, installation work, training and support, customer contact, public communication, minor civil work, and integration of data software with Town’s billing software.
The Town is expecting to complete the project within 6 to 8 years depending on annual funding provisions.
