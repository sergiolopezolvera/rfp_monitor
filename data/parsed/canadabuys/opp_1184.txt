Description:
The scope of this requirement is for the provision of IM/IT professional services required to plan, organize, manage, design and implement an Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. The services to be provided by the Contractor under this Contract will be delivered on an “as-and-when-requested” basis as described in the SOW and includes the following work streams and categories of services:
1. Stream 3 - Information Management/Information Technology Services
• I.4. Database Modeller/Information Management Modeller (Level 3)
• I.5. Information Management Architect (Level 3)
• I.10. Technical Architect (Level 3)
2. Stream 6 - Cyber Protection Services
• C.2. Information Technology Security Methodology, Policy and Procedures Analyst (Level 3)
• C.3. Information Technology Security Threat and Risk Assessment and Certification and Accreditation Analyst (Level 3)
• C.7. Information Technology Security Design Specialist (Level 3)
