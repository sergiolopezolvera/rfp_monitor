Description:
The M.D. of Bonnyville is developing a business case to support an industrial corridor that aligns with Alberta’s goals of investment attraction, economic diversification, and job creation. The project will assess infrastructure needs—such as utilities, water, and gas—to support industrial growth and community development. It will identify suitable industry clusters, evaluate land and zoning, and analyze economic impacts. The corridor is expected to boost employment, population growth, and municipal revenues. Through strategic planning and collaboration with regional partners, the initiative will enhance investment readiness, support business retention, and foster long-term economic sustainability across the region.
