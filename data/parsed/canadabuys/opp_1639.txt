Description of the Requirement:
The Canadian Forces School of Communications and Electronics (CFSCE) locating in Kingston ON requires these services in delivering trade specific training activities. Services will include curriculum development, instruction, assessment, and program improvement to meet the operational needs of the Canadian Forces.
