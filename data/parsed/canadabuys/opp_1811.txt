Description:
Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC) is issuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) for the Translation Bureau under an existing Supply Arrangement (SA). As such, only pre-qualified suppliers who have been formally invited may access the RFP documents and submit a proposal for the provision of English-to-French translation services in the field of “Courts and administrative law” (Tier 3). One contract will be awarded and will cover a volume of up to 2,000,000 words during the initial one-year period. This bid solicitation is issued only to suppliers who are pre-qualified in the Tier 3 - field of "Courts and administrative law", from English to French, in accordance with the conditions of SA EN966-140305. The following suppliers have been invited to submit a bid :LES TRADUCTEURS REUNIS, S.C.C., SERVICES LINGUISTIQUES VERSACOM INC., FOX TRANSLATIONS LTD., LIONBRIDGE LTD., MEGALEXIS COMMUNICATIONS INC. et NATIONS TRANSLATION GROUP LP.
