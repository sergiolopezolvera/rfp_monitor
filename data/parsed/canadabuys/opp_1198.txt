Description:
This RFP is an invitation by the Town of Hinton to prospective proponents to submit proposals for Long Line Re-Painting (Asphalt).
The Town of Hinton is a municipality of approximately 10,000 people located in the Yellowhead Region of Alberta. The municipality is seeking a qualified contractor for the supply, delivery and application of long line roadway markings on asphalt for the 2026, 2027 and 2028 calendar years.
