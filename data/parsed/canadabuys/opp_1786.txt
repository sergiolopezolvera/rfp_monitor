Description of the Requirement:
AAFC seeks a single contractor to provide resources in the categories herein that are qualified to provide support in developing and implementing applications within Microsoft’s Power Platform for the Grants and Contributions Digital Platform (GCDP).
AAFC requires resources provided by the contractor to meet the requirements of the TA, and the TA evaluation criteria provided at TA issue.
