Description:
The Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) requires the services of Detention Guards on an "as and when requested basis" to assist with duties which are related to the safety and well-being of persons incarcerated in RCMP cells.
This Request for Standing Offer (RFSO) is to establish 3 Regional Individual Standing Offers (RISO). One RISO per region, as detailed below:
Alberta Region (Alberta, Northwest and Nunavut Territories);
Prairie Region (Manitoba and Saskatchewan)
Pacific Region (British Columbia and Yukon Territory)
The period of the Standing Offer will be from the date of issuance to March 31, 2029. The Standing Offer will have the irrevocable option to extend by 3 additional one year periods.
