Canada has set aside this procurement under the federal government Procurement Strategy for Indigenous Business. This set aside is a measure with respect to Indigenous peoples under Article 800 of the Canadian Free Trade Agreement (CFTA) and a measure with respect to Aboriginal peoples or a set-asides for small and minority business under the international trade agreements.
REQUIREMENT
The Canada Energy Regulator (CER) requires a contractor to provide executive search services in support of the renewal of the CER Indigenous Advisory Committee. The CER’s Board of Directors will be looking to fill vacant IAC positions in both 2026 and 2027, as the terms of its members reach maturity. The purpose of this current contract is to support filling the vacancies anticipated in 2026, with potential to support a second search in 2027, if required.
The Contractor must perform the following at minimum:
provide a targeted executive search, verification and referencing services for up to three Committee Member positions;
• provide strategic advice and support to the hiring committee, composed of CER Executives and Directors, by leading candidate screening (including Indigenous identity validation and affiliations with other Indigenous organizations), coordinating the candidate review process and providing advice on candidate on-boarding processes.
The CER Board of Directors has the final approval on IAC membership. The Contractor will work closely with the Project Authority as well as the hiring committee, comprised of CER Executives and Directors.
It is anticipated that the public call for expressions of interest would be launched in June 2026. Work must be completed by October 2026.
Depending on the recruitment plan, an additional campaign may be required in 2027.
