Department of National Defence (DND) has a requirement for four (4) vehicle mechanic technicians to assist in the support and maintenance of Tactical Armoured Patrol Vehicle (TAPV) fleet vehicles at Garrison Petawawa. The technicians will be required at Garrison Petawawa where tasks may include, but are not limited to, conducting inspections, diagnosis, and repairs of TAPV fleet vehicles and their equipment.
