Description:
The purpose of this RFP is to solicit proposals from qualified Suppliers for the provision of a specialized business continuity management tool. The WCB is seeking to purchase a software or service solution that will enable the WCB to streamline planning, enhance real-time incident response, and improve targeted and crisis communication both internally and externally.
The selected solution will replace the existing planning, incident management and emergency communications currently in existing tools such as MS Teams, Word/Excel, Clarity and NetSupport Notify. This initiative seeks a tool that operates outside our core environment ensuring we can effectively respond even if our networks or systems are compromised. A cloud-based, independent solution will allow key stakeholders to access plans, coordinate responses, and communicate effectively, ensuring quick recovery and continued delivery of critical WCB services, through any business disruption.
Suppliers are invited to propose solutions that demonstrate proven capabilities in business continuity management software or service solutions.
