Title: Supply and Installation of Heavy-Duty Dump Boxes, Scrapers, and Plows

Status: Open

Closing date: 2026-06-11

Reference number: RFQ 26-0052

Bid type: RFQ-Low Bid

Description:
The City of Saskatoon requires the supply and installation of the following:
Three (3) units of front plows and 15 ft dump boxes for 60,000 lb 2027 Kenworth T480 trucks.
Three (3) units of underbody scrapers and 15 ft dump boxes for 54,000 lb 2027 Kenworth T480 trucks; and
One (1) 12 ft dump box for 30,000 lb 2027 Freightliner M2-106 truck.
