Title: Trailers with Related Equipment and Services

Status: Open

Closing date: 2026-06-25

Reference number: RFP 062526

Bid type: RFP - General

Description:
Sourcewell,
a State of Minnesota local government unit and service cooperative,
is requesting proposals for Trailers with Related Equipment and Services to result in a procurement solution for use by its Participating Entities. Sourcewell Participating Entities include thousands of governmental, higher education, K-12 education, nonprofit, tribal government, and other public agencies located in the United States and Canada.
Sourcewell encourages the use of Small, Minority-owned, Women’s Business Enterprise, Veteran-owned Business Enterprise (S/M/WBE/VBE) suppliers both as prime and subcontractors and encourages the participation of these suppliers in the solicitation process.
A full copy of the Request for Proposals can be found on the Sourcewell Procurement Portal [
https://proportal.sourcewell-mn.gov
]. Only proposals submitted through the Sourcewell Procurement Portal will be considered. Proposals are due no later than June 25, 2026, at
3:30 p.m. Central Time,
and l
ate proposals will not be considered.
