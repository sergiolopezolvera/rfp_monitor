Title: Road Repair Service

Status: Open

Reference number: RFSQRF2025138

Bid type: RFSQ (Roster Framework)

Description:
The City of St. John's requires a Roster Framework for
Road Repair Service
. Those submissions that are deemed to be compliant with the pre-qualification process will be considered accepted submissions. The successful pre-qualified respondent(s) will be placed on a Roster which City staff will utilize to solicit competitive quotations for specific projects.
Suppliers that qualify to be included in a
Road Repair Service
Roster will be contacted on a rotating basis for a Second-Stage Competitive Process (otherwise referred to as a Request for Quotations (RFQ)) for
Road Repair Service
. All requests will be for a lump sum price. Quotes may need to be received in a short timeframe (within 12 to 24 hours as specified for each request). It is anticipated the successful bidder will be given approval to proceed soon after the quote deadline and will be required to begin work as outlined in the RFQ.
