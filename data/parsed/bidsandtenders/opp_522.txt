Notice to Bidders
City of Moose Jaw
Bid for:
City of Moose Jaw Crescent View Headworks Facility
Emails containing Bids with the subject line “BID: Crescent View Headworks Facility” shall be received by AECOM Canada ULC. by email to:
60665324@aecom.com before 2:00:00 p.m., local time, on May 13, 2026.
There will be no public bid opening. Bid results will be made public upon request.
The Work generally comprises of the construction of the new Crescent View Headworks Facility in the City of Moose Jaw, Saskatchewan. The work includes:
Furnishing of all materials, plant, equipment, tools, supplies, temporary lighting and heating, transportation, labour and superintendence necessary for the construction of the work as herein specified and as shown on the drawings.
Construction new Crescent View Headworks facility complete with a Generator Building, one septage receiving station, and two bulk water truck fill stations. This work includes but is not limited to: building foundations and envelopes, mechanical, electrical and process work.
Underground utility construction including but not limited to: 650mm and 600mm force mains, 1500 mm and 900 mm sanitary sewers, sanitary truck connection chambers, flow splitting chamber, treatment bypass valve chamber, and watermains.
Earthworks on site including but not limited to; excavation and backfilling of new Headworks facility and associated site works, construction of new and parking lot, and truck turn-a-round road, and lagoon berm removal/restoration including temporary cofferdam at the Wastewater Treatment Plant Sites.
Civil site work including but not limited to: organic stripping, landscaping, topsoiling, grass seeding and fence installation.
Planning, engineering, and construction of a watertight shoring system to support excavation around the Headworks building and the removal of contaminated soils.
Remediation of contaminated soils encountered at the project site in as specified herein including the appended Phase II ESA and Corrective Action Plan.
Demolition of both the existing Crescent View Lift Station and Wastewater Treatment Plant Grit Building. This work includes Hazardous Materials identification, removal, and disposal.
Commissioning of all facilities.
Testing all materials for approval for incorporation into the work.
Cleaning up of the site.
Repair of all defects for two years after completion of the work.
