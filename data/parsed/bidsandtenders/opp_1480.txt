Description:
Oxford County intends to construct two (2) net-metering solar photovoltaic (PV) systems commencing in 2026. The project scope will consist of the installation of roof-mounted net-metering system sized and located as follows:
53 kWDC (50 kWAC) system located at the Highland Patrol Yard, 884135 Road 88, Embro, ON N0J 1J0
81.9 kWDC (57.6 kWAC) system located at the multi-unit residential building, 16 George Street, Norwich ON N0J 1P0
