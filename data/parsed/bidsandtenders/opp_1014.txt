Title: Standby Power System Facility for the Iona Island Wastewater Treatment Plant (IIWWTP) Projects

Status: Open

Closing date: 2026-06-08

Reference number: 25-290

Bid type: RFP

Description:
The purpose of this RFP is to invite Proponents to submit Proposals for construction and installation services for the Standby Power System (SPS) Facility for the IIWWTP Projects. The general scope of work of this RFP and the list of pre-purchased major equipment that will form part of the SPS facility are included below.
RFP 25-290 SPS Facility for IIWWTP Projects – General Scope of Work
Electrical
Installation of underground conduits and cable tray
Placement and connection of generators, transformers, and E-houses
Installation of power, instrumentation and control cables
Site acceptance testing, commissioning, and training for O&M staff
Shutdown and cutover activities
Mechanical
Installation of oil-water separator system
Installation of diesel fuel system
Installation of stormwater dewatering system
Civil & Structural
Trenching for electrical conduits and mechanical pipes
Construction of concrete equipment pads
Metro Vancouver pre-purchased the following major equipment that will form part of the SPS facility. The contractor will be responsible for offloading, placement, installation and connection of this equipment, as well as for site acceptance testing, commissioning, and training for O&M staff.
Standby Diesel Generators (SDGs) – 6 x 2MW containerized (i.e., shipping container type)
Electrical Houses (E-Houses) containing 12.5kV switchgear
Transformers, load banks, and neutral grounding resistors
The contractor that will be retained to construct and install the SPS will be responsible for supplying ancillary equipment and materials such as:
Medium voltage circuit breaker sections
Fuel tanks and fuel piping systems
Storm water pumping system, oil water separator, and other mechanical items
Electrical cables, cable tray, lighting, grounding, and other electrical items
Concrete pads, steel walkways, anchors, and other structural items
Grading, backfill, clear crush finishing layer, and other civil items
