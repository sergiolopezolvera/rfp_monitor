Title: Qualified Supplier List - Access Control System

Status: Open

Closing date: 2026-08-31

Reference number: RFSQ 24-0345Y2

Bid type: RFSQ

Description:
City of Saskatoon will be creating a qualified supplier list for the supply and installation of Honeywell ProWatch access control components, HID Signo readers, and peripherals. Qualified respondents will work closely with Corporate Security on installation and system testing.
This RFSQ does not apply to respondents who currently hold an active Master Framework Agreement. Only new respondents without a signed agreement should respond to this RFSQ. The City of Saskatoon reissues this RFSQ annually to allow new respondents to participate. Submissions will be evaluated as received, and awards will be made accordingly throughout the year.
