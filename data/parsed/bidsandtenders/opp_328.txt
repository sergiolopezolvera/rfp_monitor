Description:
The purpose of this Request for Pre-Qualification is to invite interested electrical contractors to submit responses indicating their interests in, and qualifications for, becoming pre-qualified to participate in future procurement opportunities for Data Networking Infrastructure Upgrades at various Island Health facilities (Geo Regions 1-4). This opportunity is open to both new firms as well as firms that have experience with Island Health projects.
