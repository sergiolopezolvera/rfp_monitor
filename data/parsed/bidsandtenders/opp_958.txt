Description:

The Town of Oakville (the town) invites tenders for goods described as Supply & Delivery of:
EXHIBIT A:
Up to Eight (8) New, Zero-Turn Front-Mounted Rotary Mowers, Winter Convertible Type.
EXHIBIT B:
Up to Two (2) New, 4 x 4, Diesel Powered, All Purpose Utility Work Vehicles.
EXHIBIT C:
Up to Two (2) New, Autonomous Robotic Turf Mowers
EXHIBIT D:
Up to Three (3) New, Electric Mowers 60" Deck:
Two (2) Side Discharge, and
One (1) Rear Discharge
EXHIBIT E:
One (1) New, Compact Tractor with 4WD Loader and 60" mid-mount mower deck Work Vehicles.
Bidders may bid on any or all Exhibits.
Bidders must be the Manufacturer or an Authorized Dealer for the items being bid and confirm as such. Failure will deem submission non-compliant.
