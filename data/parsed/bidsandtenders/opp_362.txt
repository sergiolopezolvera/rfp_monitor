Title: Oxford-on-Rideau PS - HVAC Upgrades, Boiler, Water treatment room, Solar & Renos

Status: Open

Closing date: 2026-05-25

Reference number: 26-057

Bid type: ITT

Description:
The Upper Canada District School Board invites tenders, from
pre-qualified
eligible bidders, for
HVAC Upgrades, Boiler replacement, Water Treatment room, Solar installation & Renovations
including;
Air conditioning to occupied spaces.
Relocate boiler room to main floor. New boilers.
Relocate water treatment room.
New JACE based building automation.
Install 48 KW Net Metering DC Solar PV Array.
Upgrade primary electrical feed.
Provide ventilation to existing electrical room.
Replace select light fixtures with new LED lights.
Replace existing school lettering with standard UCDSB lettering to support branding strategy.
as further described herein at:
OXFORD-ON-RIDEAU PUBLIC SCHOOL
50 Water Street, Oxford Mills, ON, K0G 1J0
Schedule
All work specified in 1.3 – Scope of Work is to commence on or about June 3, 2026.
ALL WORK AWARDED AS A RESULT OF THIS TENDER MUST HAVE REACHED SUBSTANTIAL COMPLETION ON OR BEFORE December 12, 2026.
