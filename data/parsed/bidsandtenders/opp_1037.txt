Title: Sewage Pumping Station and Forcemain Upgrades

Status: Open

Published date: 2026-04-30

Closing date: 2026-05-29

Reference number: 2026-RFSQ-55

Bid type: Request For PreQualification

Description:
This Request for Supplier Qualifications (“RFSQ”) is an invitation by the Regional Municipality of Niagara (“Niagara Region”) to prospective respondents to qualify for eligibility to provide 2026-RFSQ-55 Sewage Pumping Station and Forcemain Upgrades as further described in Section A of the RFSQ Particulars (Appendix A) (the “Deliverables”).
The objective of the work as further described within this RFSQ is to upgrade various Sewage Pumping Stations (SPS) and their respective Forcemains within Niagara Region.
Niagara Region anticipates tendering three (3) construction projects in 2026. Refer to Appendix A – RFSQ Deliverables.
The construction value and schedule for the Sewage Pumping Stations and Forcemain Upgrades included in this RFSQ are as follows:
Spring Gardens SPS and FM Upgrades: Construction value estimated at approximately $10,500,000.00 (CAD). Scheduled for tender in Q2 2026
Shirley Road Pump Station Upgrades: Construction value estimated at approximately $3,900,000.00 (CAD). Scheduled for tender in Q2 2026
City Hall SPS and FM Upgrades: Construction value estimated at approximately $4,000,000.00 (CAD). Scheduled for tender in Q4 2026
Please note that the schedule of the tenders may be subject to change.
