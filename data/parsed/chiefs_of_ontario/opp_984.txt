Title: Request for Proposals: Indigenous Videographer Species at Risk Conference Video Report

Organization: Chiefs of Ontario

Author: Charlotte Croft

Publication date: 2025-02-18

Closing date: 2025-03-04

Notice type: Request for Proposals

Category: Environment | Youth

Description:
You are invited to submit a proposal to support the Ontario Chiefs’ project to develop a high-level, professionally made 15-20-minute video that captures First Nation Youth learning about Species at Risk infused with First Nation Culture and Spirituality in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario.
Preference will be given to an Indigenous-owned and operated production company.
Submission Deadline:
March 4, 2025, at 11:50 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST)
Please submit electronically to
Angela.Trudeau@coo.org
. Submissions received after the stated deadline will not be accepted.
PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS:
All submissions must include the following:
Cover Letter;
Detailed work plan with all deliverables accounted for;
Completed fee schedule of estimated budget in alignment with deliverables;
CVs and References.
2025 RFP SARS Video Report
The interested candidate’s proposal must include the following:
A demonstration of their expertise in addressing high-level, professionally made 15–20-minute video reports to submit to funders.
A work plan in alignment with the project deliverables within the provided timeframe of March 31, 2025.
A proposed budget for the completion of the work with a budget schedule in alignment with the completion of each project deliverable for a maximum amount of $20,000
