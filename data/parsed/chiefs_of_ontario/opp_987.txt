Title: Request for Proposals: COO Education Sector - First Nations Adult Education Program Delivery and Cost Analysis Project

Organization: Chiefs of Ontario

Author: Kallie Diabo

Publication date: 2025-01-10

Closing date: 2025-01-24

Notice type: Request for Proposals

Category: Communications | Education

Description:
You are invited to submit a proposal for a project by the Chiefs of Ontario for the First Nations Adult Education Program Delivery and Cost Analysis Project.
The project will aim to:
Highlight the success of current First Nation administered adult education programs and delivery methods as currently funded by Indigenous Services Canada (ISC)
Identify other First Nation Adult Education programs and delivery methods beyond current ISC program parameters,
Provide estimated costs associated with program expansion which must also include costs for operations maintenance, and infrastructure.
Submission deadline is January 24, 2025 at 5 pm. Selection of the consultant will be awarded on January 29, 2024.
