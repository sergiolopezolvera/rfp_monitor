Title: Request for Proposals: Mental Wellness Event Planner

Organization: Chiefs of Ontario

Author: Kallie Diabo

Publication date: 2024-07-24

Closing date: 2024-08-06

Notice type: Request for Proposals

Category: Communications | Health

Description:
The Chiefs of Ontario are inviting individuals to submit a proposal to take on the event planning for an event focusing on the toxic drug crisis facing First Nations communities, to be hosted by the Chiefs of Ontario. The target date for this event is the end of November 2024.
REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
Submission Deadline:
August 6, 2024 AT 12:00PM
Eastern Standard Time (EST)
If you have further questions about the proposal form and the process, please email Tobi Mitchell, Director of Health at Tobi.Mitchell@coo.org.
