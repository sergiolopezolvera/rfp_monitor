Title: Request for Proposal: AV Services for Chiefs of Ontario 50th Anniversary Annual Chiefs Assembly

Organization: Chiefs of Ontario

Author: Kallie Diabo

Publication date: 2025-04-23

Closing date: 2025-05-01

Notice type: Request for Proposals

Category: Communications

Description:
The Chiefs of Ontario (COO) invites qualified A/V service providers to submit proposals for comprehensive audio-visual support for the Chiefs of Ontario’s 50th Anniversary Annual Chiefs Assembly. This annual assembly will bring together hundreds of participants, including First Nation leadership, Knowledge Keepers, youth, and community members from across Ontario, in a hybrid format.
The AV service provider will be responsible for setup, operation, and teardown of all audio visual and power requirements for the following event spaces:
Main assembly room
Registration and Conference Office
Submission Deadline:
May 1, 2025 AT 12:00PM
Eastern Standard Time (EST)
If you have further questions about the proposal form and the process, please email Isak Vaillancourt, Communications Manager at Isak.Vaillancourt@coo.org
