About Surrey Place
Surrey Place is a not-for-profit organization serving people of all ages with developmental disabilities, autism spectrum disorder, and visual impairments in the Toronto region and Northwestern Ontario. More information:
www.surreyplace.ca
Overview
Surrey Place is seeking quotations from qualified vendors to establish multiple vendor rosters for interpretation services to ensure comprehensive coverage across 57+ languages and service modalities.
Key Points:
Multi-vendor roster — we will award multiple vendors
Non-exclusive — no guaranteed work volume
Vendors need not provide all languages or service types
Specialized vendors are encouraged to apply
Evaluation: Pass/fail on mandatory requirements, reference verification, then award based on lowest price
Contract Structure:
Initial Term: 3 years (July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2029)
Renewal Options: One additional 3-year renewal and one 2-year renewal at Surrey Place’s discretion (maximum 8-year term)
