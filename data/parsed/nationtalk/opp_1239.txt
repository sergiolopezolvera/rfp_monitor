Description:
Algoma University is advancing the development of Makwa Waakaa’igan, an Indigenous-led cultural, academic, and community space being established in partnership with the Children of Shingwauk Alumni Association (CSAA) at the University’s Sault Ste. Marie campus, on the site of the former Shingwauk Hall Indian Residential School.
This transformative project will create a dedicated place for learning, gathering, and cultural connection on the historic Shingwauk site, while supporting Algoma University’s Special Mission of cross-cultural learning and reconciliation.
Algoma University is seeking a qualified consultant team to develop a Strategic Business Plan and Functional Program that will guide the implementation and long-term operations of Makwa Waakaa’igan. This work will involve close collaboration with University leadership, project representatives, and CSAA to ensure a sustainable, culturally grounded, and community-informed operating model.
Algoma University strongly encourages submissions from Indigenous-owned businesses and teams with demonstrated experience working with Indigenous communities and cultural initiatives.
