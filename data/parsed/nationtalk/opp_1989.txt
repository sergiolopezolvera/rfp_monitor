Description:
Overview
Gabriel Dumont Institute Training and Employment (GDITE) is seeking qualified research partners to undertake a comprehensive impact study of a Métis employment and training program delivered over the past 20 years. The program has supported thousands of Métis citizens across Saskatchewan through skills training, education pathways, employment services, and career development initiatives. For the complete requirements, download RFP Impact Study GDITE May 2026.
Purpose of the Study
Employment and economic outcomes
Education and skills development
Entrepreneurship and wealth creation
Social, family, and community well-being
Program effectiveness and evolution
Why This Matters
This study will demonstrate long-term impact, inform future Métis workforce development strategies, support policy and funding decisions, and contribute to nation-building and economic self-determination.
Scope of Work
Quantitative analysis (employment, earnings, program data)
Qualitative research (interviews, focus groups, case studies)
Economic analysis (cost-benefit or social return on investment)
Métis-informed and community-engaged research approaches
