Title: Curriculum Developer for the Indigenous Health Navigator Certification
Author: ahnationtalk
Deadline: May 18, 2026
Name: First Nations Health Managers Association (FNHMA)
Type: RFP
Region: ON
Sector: Public Sector
Category: health

Description:
The First Nations Health Managers Association (FNHMA) has issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) seeking a curriculum developer for the Indigenous Health Navigator Certification. This opportunity aims to build a comprehensive and culturally relevant curriculum that supports Indigenous health navigators in their vital role within communities. Understanding the details of this RFP is essential for organizations and individuals interested in contributing to Indigenous health education.
