Description:
Ontario Aboriginal Housing Services (OAHS) has opened a Request for Proposal for Governance Policy Review and Development.
The RFP is to cover the following areas:
Review OAHS’ existing board governance policies, and financial policies as they relate to board governance
Identify gaps and areas for improvement
Develop a comprehensive suite of updated and/or new governance policies
Ensure compliance with applicable legislation and best practices
Provide implementation guidance to support policy adoption
