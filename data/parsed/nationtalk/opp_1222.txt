The Town of Drayton Valley (as may be referred to hereinafter as the “Town”) is requesting submissions from qualified vendors with the objective of establishing a qualified contractor list for a variety of Construction Services
The nature of work consists of Construction Services including but not limited to:
a. Carpentry
b. Windows, doors, overhead commercial doors
c. Masonry
d. Finishing
e. Foundations/concrete slab/pilings
f. Roofing
g. Deck, outdoor/park structures
h. Commercial fencing
