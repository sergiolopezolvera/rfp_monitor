Description
The Town of Drayton Valley is requesting a Quotation from qualified individuals and/or businesses for the delivery of Desludging Aspenview Pond for the municipality.
The Town requires the desludging of Aspenview Pond. This includes mechanical sludge excavation, hauling sludge and other materials to a designated location for dewatering, any safety equipment, signage and traffic control as needed, and any site reclamation work necessary. This does not include trucking dried sludge to the landfill after testing. This project requires qualified applicants to retain prime contractor for the full duration of the job.
