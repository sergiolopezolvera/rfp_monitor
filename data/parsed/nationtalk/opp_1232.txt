Description
Request for Bids (RFB) for Supply and Delivery of Tube and Ankle Socks.
Scope of Work
The objective of this RFB is to invite prospective Bidders to submit Bids for the provision of tube and ankle socks for inmate use for
both the Ministry of the Solicitor General and the Ministry of Children, Community and Social Services (MCCSS) on an as-and-when required basis.
The Preferred Bidder shall be required to enter into separate agreements with each of the Ministry of the Solicitor General and MCCSS for the provision of the Deliverables in the form attached in Part 2 – Form of Agreement for this RFB.
The term of the Agreement is for four (4) years. Two (2) year initial term with two (2) additional extension terms of up to one (1) year each, at the Ministry’s discretion to extend.
