Description
Yukon Energy Corporation (“the Corporation” or “YEC”) is soliciting Proposals for the sizing, supply, installation, and commissioning of up to four (4) secondary (vertical) exhaust silencers for Units WD4–WD7 at the Whitehorse Diesel WD0/P126 facility located at the Whitehorse Rapids Generation Site in Whitehorse, Yukon, including the decommissioning and removal of the existing silencers, as further described in Section 4 – Scope of Work and Appendices 2 and 3, including delivery to Yukon Energy Corporation, #2 Miles Canyon Road, Whitehorse, Yukon, Y1A 6L4. Proponents shall provide residential-grade silencers, at a minimum, sized and supplied to meet or exceed the noise attenuation performance requirements identified in Appendix 2 – RWDI Memo (2026) for Units WD4, WD5, WD6, and WD7.
