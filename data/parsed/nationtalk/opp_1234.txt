Description
This Request for Proposals (the “RFP”) is an invitation by British Columbia Ferry Services Inc. (“BC Ferries”) to prospective proponents to submit proposals for the removal of the existing car deck fan, supply, installation and commissioning of a new reversible car deck fan (collectively the “Goods and Services”) for mv Northern Expedition (“Vessel”)
Pre-Bid Vessel Visit Meeting: May 8, 2026, 10:00:00 am Pacific Time (Attendance is mandatory for all Proponents except for proponents who attended the initial mandatory vessel visit conducted under Notice of Proposed Procurement No. 49‑2025 on April 30, 2025 – these proponents are considered to have completed the mandatory vessel visit and not required to attend on May 8, 2026. For clarification: there is one vessel visit per proponent)
Proponent’s attendees will meet at the security guard booth at the entrance for Fleet Maintenance Unit located at 12800 Rice Mill Road, Richmond, BC V6W 1A1 at 9:30 am Pacific Time. Proponent attendees must bring their own Personal Protection Equipment (PPE) – safety helmets, safety glasses, reflective vests, and safety shoes.
Proponents who are in the business of providing the Deliverables must confirm their attendance for the vessel visit via email to Elena Moskvina by 2:00 pm Pacific Time on May 4, 2026.
