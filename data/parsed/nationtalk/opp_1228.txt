Description
The Municipality of Temagami invites experienced and qualified contractors to submit quotations for the fabrication, supply, and installation of floating and fixed dock systems at the Cassels Lake Subdivision – Cassels Lake Access Point. The intent of this RFQ is to obtain a durable, safe, code-compliant, and low-maintenance docking system suitable for municipal and public use in a fluctuating water-level environment.
