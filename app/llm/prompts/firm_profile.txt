Firm Profile Document: Indigenous Communities Engagement (ICE)
1. Firm Description
Mission: To advance meaningful reconciliation between Indigenous and non-Indigenous communities by creating Pathways to Prosperity in the Indigenous Economy and improving the quality of life for Indigenous Nations through socio-economic, community, business, and project development.
Vision: A Canada where Indigenous communities thrive as central collaborators, respected partners, and empowered agents in every project affecting their communities and lands
Core Identity: A 100% Indigenous-owned advisory firm in Canada, established in 2015, that provides authentic insights and strategic solutions to bridge cultures, empower communities, and advance meaningful reconciliation.
Values:
Land Stewardship: Honoring and promoting traditional principles of environmental sustainability.
Reciprocity: Cultivating equitable relationships and shared benefits to ensure long-term prosperity for Indigenous communities.
Respect: Celebrating the rich diversity of Indigenous cultures and valuing the unique traditions of each community we serve.
Holistic Perspective: Integrating economic, social, cultural, and environmental considerations in recognition of interconnected community well-being.
Capacity Building: Empowering communities with the skills and knowledge to drive their own development.
Key Differentiators:
The firm operates at the "pace of the community," not on corporate or academic timelines.
The approach is "Indigenous-Centered," placing Indigenous knowledge, cultures, and traditions at the core of all strategies.
The firm adheres strictly to OCAP (Ownership, Control, Access, and Possession) principles for data sovereignty.
2. Detailed Services and Synonyms
This section details the services offered by ICE and the corresponding terms likely to be found on external opportunity websites.
Pillar 1: Strategic Planning, Governance, and Reconciliation
Core Service: Reconciliation Action Plans (RAPs)
Description: Guiding non-Indigenous organizations to create and implement tangible RAPs rooted in the 94 Calls to Action. This involves policy reviews, engagement strategies, and implementation support.
Likely Synonyms: Reconciliation Planning/Strategy; Indigenous Relations Policy Development; Corporate Indigenous Relations Strategy; Reconciliation Framework Development; Truth and Reconciliation Action Plan Implementation; Call to Action #92 Corporate Response; Indigenous Relations (IR) Policies; Reconciliation Readiness Assessment.
Core Service: Indigenous Participation Plans (IPPs)
Description: Designing, developing, and delivering comprehensive IPPs for corporations and the Crown to foster economic inclusion and meaningful partnerships.
Likely Synonyms: Indigenous Engagement and Participation Plans (IEPP); Indigenous Benefits Plans; Community Benefits Agreements; Indigenous Procurement Strategy; Local Indigenous Content Plan; Socio-Economic Benefits for Indigenous Peoples; Indigenous Employment and Training Plan.
Core Service: Business Advisory Services
Description: Providing strategic guidance to Indigenous businesses, entrepreneurs, and Economic Development Corporations (EDCs) to foster sustainable growth and maximize economic potential.
Likely Synonyms: Indigenous Economic Development Support; First Nations Enterprise Development; Indigenous Entrepreneurship Support; EDC Strategic Planning; Community Economic Development Initiative (CEDI); Feasibility Studies; Market Analysis; Transaction and Partnership Advisory.
Core Service: Comprehensive Community Planning (CCPs)
Description: Facilitating the creation of holistic, long-term community plans that integrate land use, capital planning, and community vision.
Likely Synonyms: Community-Led Planning; First Nations Community Planning; Holistic Community Planning; Integrated Community Sustainability Plan; Seven Generations Planning; Community Visioning and Goal Setting.
